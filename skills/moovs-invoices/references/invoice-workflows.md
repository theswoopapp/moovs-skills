# Invoice procedures

Reviewed 2026-10-16 (creation, existing-invoice PDF and non-card receipt date); other sections 2026-10-15. Follow task-authority.md for execution and exact consequential approval.

## Eligibility and creation

The public creation guide requires every trip in a reservation to be completed
and **Closed**, a remaining balance, and no attachment to an existing invoice.
A zero-balance reservation is already paid and is not invoiceable; payment status
is calculated from recorded payments, not a trip field to manually mark Paid.

1. Open **Invoices > Create Invoice** and search the intended booking contact.
2. Review eligible reservations. Have the operator select only those requested.
3. Review contact notes and due date before the operator selects **Save Invoice**.
4. Reopen it in Pending/Outstanding to verify the selected reservations and date.
   Saving has not sent it. The operator separately chooses **... > Send Invoice**,
   or downloads a PDF for their own external delivery. Review recipient first.
5. A past-due unpaid invoice moves from Pending to Outstanding.

To inspect the reservation's amount due, the public guide says open that exact
reservation, choose **Edit**, and read the amount at the top. Review only; do not
change fields, save or close a trip merely to inspect its balance.

For future billing design, the article recommends separate reservations when
waiting for all trips in a bundle would delay invoicing; this is advice, not a
requirement to split every booking. Do not duplicate or restructure existing
reservations just to make them appear. If the actual goal is upfront collection
before service, route to the separate quote workflow rather than creating an
ineligible invoice or closing unfinished work. Quote/payment access still needs
its own relevant entitlement checks; Free has no payment-link workaround.

Missing reservation: check every trip's actual completion/Closed state, balance
and existing invoice. Do not close unperformed work or delete an existing invoice
just to force eligibility. Escalate an intended rebill/restructure for review.

Source: [Create an invoice](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6621185-how-do-i-create-an-invoice)

## Download an existing invoice as PDF

1. Confirm the exact company, saved customer invoice and authorized billing
   purpose. Open that existing invoice from **Invoices**. Do not create another,
   close trips or rerun creation eligibility just to download it.
2. Review its invoice number, booking/billing contact, included reservations,
   amount and due date to avoid exporting the wrong customer's billing data.
3. The Help article's older embedded GIF offers **... > Download as PDF** next
   to **Send Invoice**, **Copy Invoice** and **Delete Invoice**. Operator chooses
   only **Download as PDF** when actual controls match. These are distinct actions;
   do not email, delete or infer what Copy Invoice does.
4. Operator checks the resulting local PDF is the intended invoice and readable.
   Download itself is not sending, customer delivery or payment. Do not upload
   the PDF/customer details into chat or public notes; any external sharing needs
   its own intended recipient and authorization.

This action is supported by public prose and the offered menu label; the GIF
actually demonstrates a send success notice, not a PDF download/file-open result.
Visible2022trip dates provide historical context, not a certified capture date
or current UI verification. Without direct permitted file/readback inspection, completion
is operator-reported. If controls or outcome differ, stop before another action.
[Public invoice Help](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6621185-how-do-i-create-an-invoice).

## Review trip detail on the existing invoice link or PDF

The July 2025 narration distinguishes the invoice link from its PDF. For the
intended existing invoice, review only the authorized output: the link is narrated
as listing order number, date/time, passenger and pickup address, with an option
to open that trip's details. Match that exact trip before opening it; this is not
permission to explore unrelated trips or share a private invoice link.

For a PDF already downloaded by the operator, the narration identifies pickup
address, passenger details, confirmation number and pickup date/time. Compare
those fields privately with the intended reservation; a PDF is a snapshot, not
proof of current trip state or payment. Do not promise every trip field appears
in either format or use a checkout link as a Free-plan workaround.

One presenter reports a failed download before another narrates a different
example. This does not verify a successful file result for the operator. Use the
separate PDF procedure above only when a download is requested; reading the link
does not require creating, sending or paying an invoice.

## Payments and external receipts

Choose existing card-payment review or external-receipt recording below. These
are different tasks; neither an email click nor a generic Paid label proves funds
were collected through Moovs.

## Review an invoice card payment

For an eligible paid-plan account, a sent invoice lets its contact open a payment
page. Card-paid invoices appear in payment history with the trips included in the
payment; the public guide calls this the Payments section in Settings. Match the
actual available view and compare the exact invoice, payment amount/status and
included trips rather than treating an email click as paid. This review authorizes
no new charge or resend and is not bank-posting verification.

## Record an already-received invoice payment

To record an already-received external payment, the public guide says open the
invoice, scroll to **Charge Customer**, choose the external method, then **Charge**.
The button wording does not turn cash/wire/etc. into an initiated transfer. Ask
the operator to verify receipt and correct amount/method before approved recording.
Do not treat this as permission to charge a saved card or bypass plan restrictions.
If the current dialog lacks the required amount/scope controls, stop and verify
current guidance rather than guessing. Refresh history and balance afterward.

Source: [Process/view invoice payments](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6703099-how-to-process-and-view-invoice-payments)

## Correct a non-card receipt's payment date

Reviewed 2026-10-16. May's public narration explicitly directs clicking an
invoice's payment and editing its received date for **non-card** receipts.
The presenter had no invoice to demonstrate; the earlier roadmap menu is an
illustration, not a filmed editor/save/readback or current label/format guarantee.
This supports conditional human completion through the identified actual form,
not an invented click sequence. Credit-card receipt dates are excluded.

1. Confirm the company, existing invoice/customer and **existing** external
   receipt: amount, cash/check/other non-card method, current date and the actual
   date received. Obtain approval for that correction and relevant accounting
   period. Do not request private receipt documents in chat or invent a date to
   force reconciliation. Confirm applicable account access/member permission.
2. Open that existing invoice in **Invoices**, then click its intended payment
   as narrated. Match the receipt's amount/method before editing; do not choose
   **Charge Customer**, record another payment or change the invoice due date.
3. The operator identifies that payment's actual **payment-date editing** control
   and opens its form within the approved task. If absent, card-only, ambiguous or restricted,
   stop for current Moovs guidance. Do not guess menu labels or use another
   payment to discover a workaround. Review the current date and the form's
   displayed date convention; resolve any unclear format/timezone before entry.
4. The operator enters only the approved received date, reviews the exact
   payment/date and completes the actual form's identified save/apply
   action. Do not invent its label, a required time field or automatic-save
   behavior. Stop before submission if its purpose or scope is unclear.
5. Reopen the **same invoice and same payment**. Compare the corrected received
   date and unchanged amount/method; review the invoice balance/status for an
   unexpected change. A form preview, toast or changed invoice due date alone is
   not the corrected receipt. If unavailable or uncertain, report that state and
   inspect the same record/ask Support; never delete/recreate, refund or recharge
   as a date fix. Without direct inspection, label readback operator-reported.

This edits a recorded received date; it does not collect/refund money or alter a
card processor's settlement date. Do not claim every report or accounting sync
has reconciled from this record alone. Free payment exclusions below still apply;
a date correction is not permission to create a restricted payment.

## Free-plan boundary

No Stripe-backed card processing or payment links on Free; digital invoices can
support outside-platform manual collection. The current payment guide also says
Free does not support marking trip payments Paid through external methods such
as cash/Zelle. Do not treat manual collection or invoice creation as permission
to record a payment through a restricted control or use an invoice as a bypass.

Sources: [Free-plan policy](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/9927108-important-updates-for-free-plan-users),
[Processing a Payment](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6415309-processing-a-payment).
