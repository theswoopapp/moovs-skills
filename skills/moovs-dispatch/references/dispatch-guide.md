## Vehicle Availability

The feature guide lists **Pro and VIP**, while the current public pricing matrix
marks Vehicle availability view for **Standard and higher**. This is conflicting
public guidance, not an entitlement grant; confirm the account's actual access
with Moovs before a gated action. Open Dispatch and use its Vehicle Availability
view control; the public guide describes a top-right chart icon. Choose the date.
Vehicles appear beside a 24-hour timeline. Hover a booking to inspect its reference,
times and route; open the booking for full trip context. Check the intended vehicle
against passenger needs and other commitments before proposing a reassignment.

## Create a Dispatch Color Rule

Color Rules are **Pro+** and saved to the Moovs user's account across devices,
not a company-wide trip-status change.

For Color Rules: hover over a supported column header > three dots >
**Color rules...**, choose value and color, optionally **Entire row**, then
operator submits **Save Rules**.
Supported columns: Trip Status, Paid Status, Close Status, Trip Classification,
Trip Type, Driver assigned/unassigned, Vehicle assigned/unassigned. Several
matching full-row rules produce stripes; cell-only rules remain cell highlights.
An upgrade prompt is not permission to buy a subscription. Reopen the rule menu
and compare the saved rule and intended cell/row display after operator save.

[List customization](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6609868), [Color Rules](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/13755303)

## Dispatch Alerts

Alerts communicate reservation requirements to drivers and administrators.
They can be selected from the reservation Dispatch block or Dispatch view.
Blue means enabled/driver-visible; grey means off. They are not merely cosmetic
formatting. The operator changes them after reviewing the actual requirement.
Do not infer a delivered push notification from an alert color.

[Dispatch Alerts](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6946029)
