# Dispatch calendar and multi-condition filter evidence

Reviewed 2026-10-15. Literal **dated** product captures are not current Operator
UI inspections. Check visible controls, role and account access before using a
historical sequence. Operator performs all date/view/filter selections; their
persistence is not universally established.

## Review the Dispatch calendar

Distinguish Dispatch's date overview, Vehicle Availability's vehicle-row timeline
and external Google events/invites. Do not install Google to inspect Dispatch or
assume these views share filters.

The [official May 10, 2021 recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sbj4wrL0zYw)
shows **Dispatch > calendar icon**, date navigation and **Day / Week / Month**
(00:12–01:03). If these match the actual screen, the operator chooses the intended
date/view; inspect the relevant event and open its trip details to compare
reference/date/time. Do not move events as navigation or assume editing permission.

The [April 8, 2021 overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1GaSbI6Mei0)
shows a calendar vehicle dropdown (00:33). Where actually offered, operator selects
the intended vehicle scope; compare visible date/vehicle and relevant trips. An
empty filtered calendar is not proof no bookings exist. These recordings do not
establish current plan access, filter persistence or current labels. Stop on a UI
mismatch rather than recreating the historical screen.

## Compose and review two AND filter conditions

Reviewed 2026-10-16. This is the complete Driver-text AND Order-Type-text review,
not a saved-filter workflow. For only one driver, prefer [current driver search](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/13293335-find-a-driver-s-trips-and-copy-their-trip-link).
That current Help body identifies a **Driver** option in the advanced Dispatch
filter drawer where available on the plan. The [official December 2024 webinar](https://fast.wistia.com/embed/iframe/g3cr6pz9bd?wtime=624s)
explicitly demonstrates composing both conditions and reviewing the narrowed grid
(10:24–10:42), but calls the rebuilt interface early. It is dated product evidence,
not current labels, account entitlement or a live-tested result.

1. Confirm company, pickup date/range, intended driver text and order-type text.
   Inspect existing search/filter scope; additional conditions also affect results.
2. Identify the actual advanced Dispatch filter form and its field, comparison,
   value and conjunction controls. For the matching dated grid, the opener is the
   **Driver column header's three-dot menu > Filter**, showing **Columns /
   Operators / Value**. Current Help calls its alternative a drawer; do not assume
   it has the historical layout. Continue only when the actual form supports both
   requested conditions and AND; otherwise pause for current instructions.
3. Explain the first predicate, then have the operator choose **Driver > contains**
   and enter the intended text in the identified form. This is a substring match,
   not a unique-driver selector. Do not edit a Driver cell or change assignments.
4. Operator adds a second condition, sets **And > Order Type > contains**, and
   enters the requested order-type text. In the dated panel this is **Add filter**;
   use actual identified equivalents, not guessed labels or demo values. Check
   that both predicates remain present and the join is AND, not OR.
5. Operator completes any clearly identified application step in the actual form;
   do not invent Apply/Save or insist on a button. The dated capture updates after
   value entry and shows narrowed rows after panel dismissal. Wait for loading to
   settle; an error, stale result or unclear application is not completion.
6. Read back the date/range, active predicates and resulting trips' actual driver
   and order-type values. A reduced row count alone is insufficient: contains can
   include another driver with matching text. An empty result is not proof no
   bookings exist outside this scope. Compare the relevant trip identity/details
   where needed; report only the observed filtered result. Without authorized
   direct readback, label completion operator-reported.

Current labels and unobserved application details remain conditional on the
identified operator form, not universal requirements to reproduce the old UI.
Other field/operator combinations, OR/grouping, removal/reset, cross-view sync,
refresh persistence and saved presets are not established here. Seek supported
instructions if those are requested; none is required to finish this two-AND
review. A narrowed grid is not a saved preset or a booking/assignment change, and
the recording's pre-existing **Saved** indicator proves no filter persistence.
