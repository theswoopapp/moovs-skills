# Passenger App connected to Operator

Reviewed 2026-10-16. This is customer-facing support for an Operator workflow,
not permission for the assistant to impersonate a passenger.

## Access and entitlement

The [Passenger App introduction](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8449114-introduction-to-moovs-white-label-passenger-app)
states it is a separate optional subscription, not included merely by holding a
Free/Standard/Pro/VIP plan. Verify actual purchase and published app for the
intended operator; do not infer from a similarly named app or base subscription.
Current account terms determine costs. Activation is a commercial/account task,
not an ordinary tutorial step. The introduction (reviewed 2026-10-16) names the
account manager or Moovs sales as the activation contact. Confirm the current
intake route; the operator controls any inquiry and commercial approval, and
contacting a team does not establish activation or a published app.

## Customer sign-in and booking

The booking article was rechecked 2026-10-16; exact signup fields and the final
submission button are not specified by that source.

1. Operator confirms the correct branded app and public store listing. The
   customer downloads it themselves. First-time customers use **Sign up**;
   returning customers use their prior booking phone number. Customer enters the
   one-time SMS code privately; never ask them to paste it into chat or forward
   it to the operator/assistant.
2. **Home > Create a Trip** starts a booking/quote journey. Explain trip type,
   order type, date/time, pickup/drop-off and passenger count, then **Next Step**.
3. Customer chooses the intended vehicle, then uses **Next Step** to review
   details. Displayed pricing depends on Operator settings. Check the offered
   quote/request or reservation flow; do not guarantee immediate priced booking.
   Later quote conversion has its own settings/eligibility scope below.
4. Hand over any payment entry and final submission. A walkthrough is not
   permission to generate a real booking or charge.
5. After an authorized task, compare the customer confirmation/reference with
   the intended Operator quote/reservation. Submission or a screenshot alone is
   not proof of payment, driver assignment or persisted Operator state.

[Booking journey](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8449159-booking-a-ride-with-the-passenger-app).

## View upcoming trips and past quotes

1. The customer uses **Home** upcoming cards and **Open Trip** for full details,
   or **Trips** for upcoming/past rides and quotes. Do not create a trip to find
   an existing booking. Check reference/date rather than guessing among cards.
2. Review pickup/drop-off, date/time and vehicle; a past quote is not a confirmed
   future reservation. When authorized, compare the intended Operator record.
   Without that inspection, describe the customer's result as operator-reported.

## Confirm an eligible quote

Public [Trips guidance](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8462049-navigating-the-app-trips)
places sent quotes and settings-dependent conversion in **Trips**. This is an
existing quote, not **Create a Trip** or a guarantee every quote is eligible.

1. With the correct branded app and customer sign-in established, customer opens
   **Trips** and the intended sent quote. Compare reference/date, pickup/drop-off,
   time, vehicle and offered price with the operator's intended quote.
2. Have the customer identify the actual offered quote-confirmation/conversion
   action and read its non-sensitive label and effect before acting. The public
   source does not name that control. If absent, ambiguous or for a different
   record, stop and reconcile eligibility/current guidance with the operator or
   Support; do not create a replacement booking or change company settings.
3. Customer opens that offered flow and checks that its actual heading/summary
   identifies this quote becoming a reservation. Review the exact itinerary,
   displayed total, required terms and any stated payment consequence. Do not
   substitute the new-booking demo or invent required fields, final buttons or
   a no-charge promise. Resolve mismatched prices or unclear effects before submit.
4. Customer privately completes only the fields actually required, including
   sensitive payment entry if requested, and personally makes the intended final
   confirmation once. Pause inspection for private entry. If the outcome is
   unclear, inspect before retrying; opening the flow is not confirmation.
5. Customer reopens the resulting trip in **Trips**; operator compares the
   corresponding reservation, reference, itinerary and status with the approved
   quote. Report conversion only from resulting state, not a toast or viewed
   quote. Without authorized readback label the outcome passenger/operator-reported;
   reservation status alone proves neither payment nor assignment.

**Residual gaps:** exact app controls, all eligibility rules and payment/validation
contracts are not published here. The conditional human-owned procedure above
supports an offered eligible flow, not a universal fixed click sequence.

## Request a trip change

Public [Trips guidance](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8462049-navigating-the-app-trips)
identifies **Trips** as the place for reservation review and change requests.
A customer request is not an operator decision or proof of an applied itinerary.

1. Customer uses their own account, opens **Trips**, and identifies the intended
   reservation/trip. Compare reference/date and current itinerary; agree exactly
   what is requested, including date/time, addresses and stop order as applicable.
2. Customer identifies the offered change-request action for that reservation
   and its actual label/effect before using it. Public Help does not name the
   opener. If no clear request route is offered, stop and ask the operator/Support
   for the current supported path; do not guess a menu, cancel/rebook or use an
   operator trip editor as if it were the customer's request form.
3. In the offered flow, have the customer identify its heading and reservation
   summary, distinguish requesting from immediately applying changes, and review
   the actual editable fields and any cutoff, price or notification warning.
   Customer supplies only the intended change in the fields actually offered;
   do not promise unsupported fields or a universal cutoff/acceptance rule.
4. Customer reviews the proposed itinerary and personally submits the intended
   request once using the actual current control. Unclear scope/effect means
   pause before submission; unclear outcome means inspect before another attempt.
5. Customer reopens the trip/request and reports the displayed result. Operator
   checks that exact reservation for a pending proposal or actual changed values.
   Compare the requested dates/times/stops with persisted state before saying
   applied. Report submitted/pending/applied/unknown separately; an unchanged
   itinerary does not prove the request failed or authorize resubmission. Without
   authorized readback, label the outcome passenger/operator-reported.

**Residual gaps:** public Passenger App prose does not establish exact request
controls/fields, cutoff or automatic-application/acceptance/notification contract.
This conditional request procedure does not include deciding/applying/declining
an operator-side proposal or changing company-wide policy.

## Edit contact methods and personal notes

1. The customer opens **Me/Profile** and reviews the intended email/phone or
   personal notes/preferences. Changes are profile-wide, not one-trip overrides.
2. Customer edits only the agreed fields using the current offered controls.
   The source does not name a universal save button; do not invent one.
3. Customer reopens the profile to compare the saved contact method/note with the
   intended change. Inspect only with authorized access and minimize personal
   data; otherwise mark the result operator-reported. Do not infer notification
   delivery or that an existing trip's instructions changed from a profile save.

## Manage profile payment methods

The [interface guide](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8449139-navigating-the-app-interface)
places payment methods under **Me**; [Profiles](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8462051-navigating-the-app-customer-profiles)
documents **Payment Methods**, adding a new card and reviewing/updating saved
methods. These are documented actions, not asserted literal current button labels.

1. In the correct branded app/account, customer opens **Me**, then identifies
   their profile's payment-method section. Agree whether the task is adding a
   method, reviewing it or updating the intended existing method; not charging,
   choosing a booking payment, assigning it to a passenger or changing other cards.
2. Customer identifies the actual offered add or existing-method update action
   and its effect. Have them check the form's heading/account context and intended
   masked method privately. Do not publish card identifiers or a screenshot.
   If no matching action/form exists, stop for current guidance/Support rather
   than transplanting **Add Card** controls from browser Portal or booking demos.
3. Pause all inspection while the customer privately enters required card/wallet
   information and personally completes the intended save/confirmation using the
   actual offered controls. Never collect numbers/CVCs or assume leaving a field
   is unsaved; do not test with a charge. Explain any displayed consequence before
   they act; unresolved errors/effects mean stop, not repeated submissions.
4. Customer reopens **Me** payment methods and privately compares the intended
   addition or changed values with the list/details; report only whether the
   intended method/change persisted, without card data. This is customer-reported
   unless independently observed through explicitly authorized, non-sensitive
   access. An opened form or toast is not readback; unknown outcome means inspect
   before retrying, not add a duplicate.

**Residual gaps:** no supported-wallet matrix, universal validation/authorization
result, removal/default-method flow or charge authority is established by these
sources. Do not turn add/review/update into those separate unsupported operations.

## Review an upcoming trip's driver and map

The undated official-linked [Passenger App demo, Flow2](https://capture.navattic.com/cm8ssihvk000l03l43iyn5t9o)
provides this bounded support path through edited image-based training. It is
not a current app inspection, coherent live transaction capture or proof that
tracking/contact is enabled on every trip. Booking-context card scenes do not
establish Profile method management or a charge/no-charge contract.

1. With the intended operator's published Passenger App and the passenger's own
   authorized sign-in already established, have the passenger open the upcoming
   trip from **Home** or **Trips**. Support must not impersonate the passenger.
2. Privately compare the intended trip, vehicle and driver before using contact
   or tracking. Do not copy driver phone numbers, precise locations or customer
   trip details into chat/public notes.
3. The demo offers **View map** for the driver heading to pickup. Have the
   passenger use that offered view and report only the needed map/status outcome.
   A map or ETA display is not proof of GPS freshness, on-time arrival, actual
   boarding or completed service; no refresh interval or accuracy is established.
4. If contact is requested, the passenger reviews the intended driver and
   personally uses the offered message/call action. Do not click it to discover
   whether it sends immediately, call/text for them, or infer delivery from an
   opened contact option.
5. If the expected trip/driver/map is absent or differs, stop before contact or
   changing settings. Verify the intended trip and supported current app behavior
   with the operator/Support; do not start a driver trip, change location permissions
   or create a booking as a test. Label unobserved outcomes passenger/operator-reported.
