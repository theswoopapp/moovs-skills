## CRM campaigns

Confirm CRM access, intended audience/segment, business purpose and appropriate
contact permissions before discussing a campaign. Draft content is not permission
to enroll contacts or launch a sequence. Review recipient selection, sender and
reply-to, unsubscribe behavior, schedule/timezone and each step before the operator
submits. Read back scheduled/sent status separately. The CRM FAQ (reviewed
2026-10-16) allows post-launch edits only for steps that have not occurred;
editing the campaign does not resend an already-sent email/text. Use the lifecycle
checks in the linked walkthrough; do not relaunch to test an edit.

Use [the public-demo walkthrough](crm-workflows.md) for segments, templates,
campaign construction, start/schedule and triggers. The official product page
links that anonymously accessible demo; it is still not proof of live account
access. Ask Support when actual controls differ; do not invent selectors or
silently authenticate a marketing domain. Preserve unsubscribes; do not offer
legal compliance guarantees.

The FAQ (reviewed 2026-10-16) recommends a separate marketing domain to reduce
risk to the main domain's reputation. It describes a configurable reply-to and
automatically handled unsubscribes in outbound marketing. These service
descriptions establish neither exact current settings nor guaranteed
inbox placement or legal compliance; do not infer setup controls from them.

## Customer Reviews

From the intended reservation/trip, **Links > Trip Review** provides its review
link. The operator decides whether and how to share it; copying is not sending.
Keep it out of public outputs. The public guide (reviewed 2026-10-16) says submitted
reviews generate an email notification and appear in **Moovs Market > Customer
Reviews**. Inspect only necessary existing records: total reviews, average rating,
feedback/rating, trip number and date received. Privately compare the intended
trip's feedback and date; receipt of an email is not proof of a public posting.

The guide describes a feedback prompt for 1–4 stars and a redirect to the preferred
public review platform for 5 stars. This is documented behavior, not a strategy
recommendation or external-platform policy endorsement. A request or redirect is
not a posted review or guaranteed rating. Do not suggest fake reviews or submit
one to test routing. The operator provides their own verified review destination;
never reuse article example business links. January's dated narration adds the
Moovs-side sequence: choose the intended review platform, enter its verified ID
in **Corporation ID**, then operator selects **Save** and checks the validation
result. April repeats identifier entry and Save. Reopen the setting and compare
the intended platform/destination; validation alone is not a posted review.
These captions do not establish current third-party screens or identifier grammar.
If the actual field or validation differs, obtain current supported guidance
before editing.

## Internal-feedback-only goal

Ask whether the operator wants private trip feedback or an external review
redirect. April explicitly allows bypassing the external review platform; January
also distinguishes Moovs feedback from an actual public posting. Do not require
external account linking, Corporation ID or a public review destination for an
internal-feedback-only goal. Current Customer Reviews Help documents feedback,
internal review readback and platform identifiers, but not the exact no-provider
enablement/settings. Obtain that supported setup before claiming automation is
ready; do not invent a None selector, unlink an existing provider or promise
anonymous feedback. The April narration ties feedback to the trip.

For already collected feedback, use the Customer Reviews readback above. That
established review procedure is not blocked by the missing no-provider setup;
a review stored in Moovs is not an externally published review.

## Automated Trip Review URL

Manual **Links > Trip Review** sharing is not an enabled completion automation.
January/July demonstrate enabling **Customer Reviews** in Moovs Market and
reviewing the destination for an external-review goal, then editing the
**Trip Completed** SMS in Notifications/Notification Center with the **Trip Review
URL** Smart Key. July explicitly says it is not inserted by default.

Confirm paid SMS/notification access (Free excludes SMS), review product access,
recipient/trigger configuration and content before operator changes. Review the
approved destination only for an external-review goal; for internal-only feedback,
resolve the no-provider setup gap above rather than requiring external linking. Enablement needs exact approval; template changes and saves follow task authority and
can affect future real trips. Reopen the saved template to check the Smart Key,
then preview permitted synthetic content and destination without sending or
changing a trip's status to trigger it. Template saved is not delivered review
request, public review or compliance certification. Do not insert a real trip URL
as a fixed template value or send an unsolicited test to customers.

## Referral dashboard

Public [referral guidance](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/9889098-moovs-referral-program-help-guide)
uses **? > Refer a Friend** and a personal referral form/dashboard. Reviewed
2026-10-16: the **operator being referred** must be a ground transportation
operator currently in business, regularly completing trips and not already using
Moovs. An existing business relationship is recommended, not required. Operator
checks applicable terms,
including the conflict below, before privately sharing their own link. The personal
dashboard shows referral progress and credit status; a shared link is not earned
or applied credit.
Do not contact prospects, promise credits without qualifying conditions, publish
private links or claim a credit is applied without account evidence.

## Referral landing-offer conflict

Reviewed 2026-10-16. The undated [invite page](https://www.moovsapp.com/invite)
advertises $200 after signing up and completing ten rides; [refer](https://www.moovsapp.com/refer)
redirects there with the same captured body. These are one marketing offer, not
two features. The [published Help guide](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/9889098-moovs-referral-program-help-guide)
instead describes signup through the personal referral form plus three paid
months, followed by $200 credit for both parties. Disclose the conflicting
qualification language; do not combine the conditions or promise cash/credit.
Use the existing personal dashboard route and confirm applicable terms with Moovs
before the operator relies on either offer. Landing-page access is not a signup,
referral attribution, earned-credit readback or partner commission payment.
