# Managed marketing service intake

[Pricing](https://www.moovsapp.com/pricing) and the [marketing overview](https://www.moovsapp.com/moovs-marketing)
advertise SEO, Pay Per Click, custom websites and a CMO package. These are service
offerings, not proof of a self-serve campaign screen or an active account contract.
Reviewed 2026-10-15. No purchase, ad spend, account authorization or campaign action.

## Identify the service and approved brief

- **SEO:** search/site optimization; a website launch is not proof SEO work is
  ordered, completed or improving rankings.
- **Pay Per Click:** managed Google Ads; GTM's reservation event is not an active
  campaign, actual payment revenue or an approved advertising budget.
- **CMO:** the overview includes SEO, PPC, social and email marketing. It is not
  identical to the CRM email builder or permission to post/send from accounts.
- **Custom website:** a design/hosting service is distinct from an operator's
  existing website editor. Confirm contracted deliverables and approval process.

Clarify the intended service/change, business market, public site, goal and
decision owner. Draft a brief/inquiry for operator review only. Have the operator
confirm package, deliverables, ownership/access, recurring fees versus ad spend,
approvals and reporting definitions with Moovs. Do not promise bookings, ranking,
ROI, a universal price or a particular account manager from marketing examples.

Exact account authorization, campaign launch/budget editing, content approval,
report access and cancellation procedures are not established by these overview
pages. Request supported instructions before any such action. Never request ad,
social or website credentials, submit the inquiry, accept terms, authorize a
provider, increase spend or publish content. All external actions stay with the
operator in a separately approved service workflow. A draft/brief, contracted
service, approved campaign, live placement and business result are separate states.
