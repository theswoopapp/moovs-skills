# CRM workflow coaching

Reviewed 2026-10-15. Public demo procedures, not a live-account acceptance test.
Confirm company, CRM entitlement, member access and requested business outcome.

## Safety and scope

Follow task-authority.md: coach HOW requests; execute ordinary approved UI steps
for DO requests, including auto-save. Exact audience/content/timing approval is
required before sends, scheduling or activation; exact scope before deletion or
connection changes. Do not launch merely to demonstrate. Without an authorized
browser, guide manually and label results operator-reported.

Safety recommendations: review audience authorization, exclusions/unsubscribes,
sender identity, links, timing/timezone and existing enrollment before submission.
Do not expose customer lists, private links or credentials. Use synthetic preview
content. A segment change can affect an automation's audience; inspect linked
campaigns/triggers before the operator changes or deletes it. Do not assume a
specific suppression or retroactivity rule the demo does not document. The bounded
May re-entry example below is not a universal event contract for every trigger.

## Choose the documented workflow

1. **Segments:** use AI audience instructions and refine its proposed conditions,
   or name a manual segment and add conditions. Review filters, preview matching
   customers privately, then operator saves. The library supports editing/deleting.
2. **Email templates:** assemble content blocks manually, use a recommended design,
   or describe the email to AI with an authorized business website for branding.
   Refine, reopen, manually edit or copy a template in the library.
3. **Campaigns:** select Create, name it, choose everyone, a segment or specified
   customers; build emails with templates and intervening waits. Review and save.
   Start sends immediately; Schedule selects a future date/time. These are separate
   consequential actions, not synonyms for saving. The library supports pausing.
4. **Triggers:** name a trigger, choose its event, such as customer creation or
   entering a selected segment, and construct its email flow. Save first; Activate
   enables automatic messages. Review audience and event scope before activation.
5. **Results and lifecycle:** campaign/trigger libraries distinguish draft, active
   and completed entries and support management/deletion. Inspect opens, clicks
   and engagement for the intended item, without equating them with bookings.
6. **Reviews:** operator connects the intended Google/Yelp business accounts;
   review, reply and review-link sharing are separate tasks. Never publish a reply
   or send a review request merely because a connection succeeded.

For launched campaigns, the [CRM FAQ](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/10771431-crm-faq)
(reviewed 2026-10-16) allows editing a step only if it has not occurred. Inspect
that step's state before the operator edits/saves with the offered controls.
An already-sent email/text is not triggered again by editing the campaign; do not
relaunch or resend to test an edit. Reopen the intended campaign and compare the
changed future step and lifecycle state without treating earlier sends as recalled.

A promo code written into AI-generated email copy is not a created or validated
offer. May explicitly leaves code creation as a separate manual task. Before any
launch, have the operator compare the proposed text with the approved saved code,
discount, date basis and usage limits using the [Promo Codes guide](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/9084919).
If no matching code exists, explain the separate offer-creation decision rather
than creating it or sending the campaign implicitly.

## Test an email and inspect recipient results

May's dated narration describes choosing a test recipient before a test send.
Confirm CRM/sender access, intended email step, approved content and the operator's
own agreed test address; a test is still a real message. After exact recipient/content approval, select
that recipient and submit the offered test-send action once in execution mode. Check the existing test
outcome before any retry; test submission is not launch, scheduling or delivery.
For an existing finished campaign, open **Campaigns**, choose the intended item
and inspect its delivery/open/click results, then only the needed recipient's
available receive/open details. Report what that record shows, not guaranteed
inbox placement, human reading, consent, bookings or revenue. Do not send or
relaunch a campaign to populate its results.

## Read back without creating traffic

After the operator acts, reopen the exact saved item and compare intended name,
conditions/audience, content, steps/waits, event and visible lifecycle state as
applicable. A draft is not active; a scheduled launch is not a sent message.
Do not infer delivery, conversion or review-platform compliance from metrics.
On an uncertain outcome, inspect existing state before another save or launch.

The demo establishes these workflow choices, not every validation rule, account
permission, scheduling timezone or unsubscribe behavior. If current controls
differ, read current public guidance or draft a Support question; do not invent
missing controls or use a live send to find out.

## Brand Kit setup and replacement

The May demonstration uses **CRM settings > Brand Kit > New Brand Kit** and the
operator's business website URL to propose logos, images, colors, email header
and footer. Confirm company, CRM access, authorized website and intended kit
before operator generation. Do not reuse a webinar business or scrape unrelated
customer websites to obtain branding.

Review proposed assets, text/links, colors and header/footer before operator edits
or **Replace existing**. Replacement is consequential shared branding, not a
preview. Reopen the saved kit and compare agreed assets/content. A permitted
synthetic unsent template can check intended presentation; do not send a campaign
or promise existing/sent emails were retroactively rebranded.

## Dynamic segment re-entry

May's illustrated inactivity-segment trigger sends when a contact matches/joins,
stops qualifying after they book, and can send **again** if they later re-enter.
This narrows the generic uncertainty for that demonstrated pattern; it does not
prove universal duplicate suppression, every event's re-entry rules or timezone.

Before operator activation, review conditions, linked template, existing activity
and repeat-message consequences. A condition edit can change who qualifies.
After operator save/activation/pause, reopen the trigger and compare conditions,
event, lifecycle state and available execution history. Do not enroll real people,
alter their history or launch messages to test re-entry. Saved is not necessarily
active, and an execution entry is not proof of customer delivery.
