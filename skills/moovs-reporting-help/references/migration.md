# Company data migration preparation

Reviewed 2026-10-15. [Public pricing](https://www.moovsapp.com/pricing) advertises
data-migration assistance and Enterprise migration services. It does not prove a
company has an approved migration, fixed timeline, import schema or cutover plan.

## Scope migration before any transfer

Distinguish full company migration from AI trip upload, a Shuttle CSV import or
an ordinary report export. Identify source system, intended destination company,
record categories/date scope and the desired operational cutover. Confirm the
contracted service, responsible Moovs contact and the business's decision owner.
Do not promise marketing's timing/parallel-run language as an account commitment.

The reviewed public sources do not establish complete field mappings, secure
transfer instructions, credential/payment-method portability, duplicate rules,
partial-failure handling, cutover acceptance or rollback. Obtain the supported
Moovs migration procedure before requesting source files or directing an import.
Do not invent templates or treat a successful upload as completed migration.

Prepare a non-sensitive question/approval checklist: included/excluded records,
source identifiers and duplicate policy, dates/timezones, dependency order,
reconciliation criteria, parallel operation, communications and recovery owner.
These are planning recommendations, not asserted Moovs migration features.
Use task-authority.md within the separately approved migration process for
source access, exports, uploads and acceptance checks. Keep exports private in the
approved destination; no broad customer lists, payment data, source credentials
or unnecessary sample records in chat or public artifacts.

Completion needs the agreed target counts, sample business-state checks and
open-error disposition against the approved scope; an upload toast or reported
cutover is not independent acceptance. Do not delete source data, cancel the old
service or send customer notices as part of preparation.

## Conflicting marketing timing is not a commitment

Reviewed 2026-10-16. The [June 9, 2026 limo-software post](https://www.moovsapp.com/post/the-best-limo-software)
says most migrations take a couple of weeks, then its FAQ says 1-2 days including
import, training and go-live. The [May 20, 2026 comparison](https://www.moovsapp.com/post/limo-anywhere-alternative)
also says a couple of weeks. Retain this conflict: neither duration is a
company-specific approved timeline. Obtain the agreed scope/owner/dates before
making commitments. All-in-one/one-price and no-downtime prose on these pages
also do not override actual plan/add-on terms or establish an uptime guarantee.
