# Advertised AI chat suggestions and routine responses

Reviewed 2026-10-16. The [December 4, 2024 AI post](https://www.moovsapp.com/post/using-ai-for-chauffeured-transportation-check-out-moovs)
advertises AI-powered chat suggestions and a bot handling routine inquiries,
including booking/FAQ assistance. This is distinct from a human's ordinary Chat
reply and from the separately described AI Voice phone service.

**Documentation gap:** current availability and the supported suggestion/reply
configuration and use procedure are not established. Ask Moovs which product
and current instructions apply; do not invent an AI Reply button, connect a
third-party model, turn on auto-send or assert Chat already answers autonomously.
The operator reviews recipients/content and performs all sends. A generated
answer is not proof a customer received it or a booking was changed.

The [August 19, 2025 AI-operations advice](https://www.moovsapp.com/post/how-to-incorporate-ai-into-your-transportation-operations)
suggests uploading customer documents, emails and statements to external AI.
That advice does not grant consent or override the sensitive-data boundary. Use
fictional or appropriately redacted examples, not real customer emails, identity
documents, full card images/numbers, bank details, credentials or statements.
