# Affiliate discovery and farm-out

Reviewed 2026-10-16 against11complete public Help bodies. Operator creates/links partners and submits every farm-out,
price/assignment change, request, PDF/link share or message.

## Find and evaluate a partner

**Contacts > Affiliates** lists partners; **Map View** searches service area.
Select a red pin > **View in Moovs** to inspect profile/contact/vehicles/pricing.
The list is alphabetical; **View** can exclude External/GriddNet where shown.
The public guide describes **Map View** as Moovs affiliates only, not the whole
external-partner list.
A map result is not independent availability, insurance or service acceptance.
Review actual vehicle/type/capacity, relevant requirements, proposed price and
partner agreement before proposing a farm-out.

[Service-area search](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6609938), [Moovs affiliate workflow](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6319067)

## External partner setup

Reviewed 2026-10-16 against both complete public creation guides and their literal
Help form/linking media, not the current authenticated app. An external affiliate
is outside the Moovs network; this is business-profile and requested association
setup, not a trip request, driver dispatch or payment.

### Create the business profile

1. Confirm the intended company, outside-Moovs partner and requested setup.
   Open **Contacts > Affiliates** and check for the same partner before creating
   another profile. Resolve ambiguous identity; do not duplicate it as a test.
   Check actual access/controls; an absent option needs the owner or Support,
   not a permission/plan change. No trip or payment is needed to create a profile.
2. Open **Create Affiliate**. The operator enters the actual required business
   information and any agreed optional details. Dated Help forms show affiliate
   name, contact name, email, phone and address with asterisks, plus Internal
   Notes. Follow the actual form's required fields; do not invent missing data
   or copy the public demonstration's example business into the account.
3. Review those details and the intended business, then the operator submits
   **Create Affiliate**, the explicit Help submission action. Do not substitute
   a guessed Save control or continue through conflicting current validation.
4. Return to the affiliate list and reopen the intended profile. Compare saved
   business details with the agreed entry, not merely a toast. If creation is
   uncertain, inspect current records before retrying. Without browser readback,
   call completion operator-reported, not independently verified.

This completes ordinary profile creation when that is the request. Missing
partner drivers/vehicles do not justify inventing profiles or blocking creation:
the later guide expressly allows profile creation without them. For requested
associations, continue below rather than stopping at the newly created profile.

[Creation and submission](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6319199), [External profile and use](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/7985307), [Member access](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/9456923)

### Create missing profiles only when requested, then link them

1. Establish the actual partner-owned driver(s) and vehicle(s) to associate;
   check existing profiles first. Obtain missing details privately from the
   partner through the operator, not an assistant-sent message. Do not link a
   look-alike driver or borrow/reassign another partner's vehicle.
2. If an intended driver is missing, the operator opens **Settings > Drivers**
   and the creation form. Public Help requires first name and mobile number;
   enter the verified number privately, plus known agreed optional details.
   The operator completes the actual form's creation action and reopens the
   intended saved driver. Do not select a reservation Dispatch driver: that
   assignment sends an accept/decline request and is not profile setup.
3. If an intended vehicle is missing, the operator opens **Vehicles** and its
   creation form, supplies actual vehicle details/required fields and only
   approved rates, policies, features and public-facing material, then completes
   the offered creation action. The public vehicle guide explains name/type,
   passenger capacity excluding the driver, actual plate/color, VIN, policies
   and photos; initial **Active** must be on and can affect customer-facing
   visibility. Review that effect before creation, not by test toggles. Reopen
   and compare the saved intended vehicle before linking it; stop when required
   facts or intended visibility/rates are unknown rather than inventing them.
4. In the intended external affiliate profile, the operator uses **Link Vehicle**,
   searches/selects the intended vehicle and confirms the actual linking form.
   The dated literal Help media shows an **Add Vehicle** dialog and submission,
   then the linked vehicle card. This is association of an existing vehicle,
   not permission to create a duplicate or reassign another affiliate's vehicle.
5. The operator uses **Link Driver** and selects the intended existing driver.
   Dated literal Help media shows the dropdown selection writing the link and
   a Saving/Saved indicator; treat selection as the mutation, not a preview.
   Use current supported controls if the form differs; stop on an unclear effect.
6. Reopen the same affiliate and compare each requested driver and vehicle with
   the approved identities. For the vehicle, **Vehicles > External Affiliate**
   also locates its associated affiliate beside the vehicle name in the older
   guide. A vehicle linked to one external affiliate cannot serve another per
   that guide. Do not unlink/reassign it merely to troubleshoot one trip.

### Finish setup without promising universal farm readiness

The older 6319199 guide requires a linked vehicle to farm out and a linked driver
before assigning that driver to a trip; the later 7985307 guide calls both
nonessential to creating and using external affiliates. Requested profile/link
setup can be completed and read back without deciding that universal requirement.
Do not label every no-link profile farm-ready or require speculative links to
create it. For an actual farm/assignment path with unclear current validation,
obtain the current supported requirement before its consequential action.

Once a trip is separately farmed, the later guide describes an email whose
**View Farm Request** opens details for the intended external recipient to enter
manually in their own system. Additional driver details may need to be returned
privately and linked by the operator. Driver App messages use the originating
company's name, not the external affiliate's. These downstream descriptions do
not mean profile/link creation sent, delivered or accepted a trip, activated the
Driver App, or configured payments/notifications. Check the requested capability's
purchased access separately; Free Help excludes payment processing, SMS and named
customer notifications. Do not buy an upgrade or bypass a limit during setup.

[Conflicting farm-use requirements](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6319199), [Later use and communications](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/7985307), [Free-plan boundaries](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/9927108)

## Farm-out a Moovs trip

1. Confirm exact trip, direction, current farm status and intended partner.
2. Trip action bar **View More > Farm-out Trip**, then **Choose a Vehicle**.
   Public guide sorts choices near pickup and supports vehicle-type/name filtering.
3. Review actual vehicle, affiliate, farm-out price and internal affiliate note.
   Operator fills/submits **Farm-out**; assistant never sends a demonstration.
4. Requests require receiving affiliate acceptance or denial and email is sent.
   Read current status before treating the trip as covered or resending.
5. Farm-out section controls affiliate dispatch; available driver/vehicle choices
   are associated with that partner. Operator reviews them before changing.
6. Monitor actual trip status; public guidance describes synchronized updates
   from partner driver/operator. Do not infer work is complete from request sent.

[Farm-out and monitoring](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6319067), [Workflow types](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6319043)

## Share affiliate trip details

For the intended trip, use **Links > Passenger** or **More > View PDF Options >
Affiliate PDF**, depending on the requested format. Review the exact recipient
and minimum necessary trip contents before the operator shares privately.
Copying/opening is not sending, acceptance or proof the partner can access it.
For a requested send, ask the recipient to confirm reception/access separately;
do not put the link or PDF into assistant chat/public notes.

[Sharing options](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6319067)

## Reconcile existing partner work

**Contacts > Affiliates > partner > Trips**. Check date, confirmation, Farm-in/
Farm-out direction, amount and paid status. External trips can show Not Paid even
when settled outside Moovs. Do not trigger a new payment to clear that label.
Use approved internal accounting evidence without putting sensitive details in
chat; financial actions remain operator-controlled. For matching outside-settlement
evidence or an internal reminder, use the bounded review in
[inbound and settlement guidance](inbound-settlement.md#review-an-external-affiliates-settlement-evidence);
that does not establish a payable-recording or payout workflow.

[Review affiliate trips](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8268255).
