# Subscription billing and disputes

Reviewed 2026-10-16. Follow task-authority.md for task-scoped execution and exact
financial/evidence approval; sensitive entry stays private.

## Moovs subscription card or failed renewal

These are cards paying for the software, not customer cards on reservations.
The published route is **Settings > Billing & Plans > Payment Method**.
Use the specific add/default/remove or failed-renewal scope below. Operator
handles private card details and sensitive-form submission; ordinary masked-method
changes follow exact task approval. No diagnostic test charge.

## Add a subscription payment method

Operator uses **Add payment method**, enters the required card details privately
and selects **Add**. Return to Payment Method and compare only the intended
masked saved method; adding it does not prove it is DEFAULT or a renewal succeeded.

## Choose the subscription default card

With multiple saved cards, operator opens the intended non-default card's menu >
**Make default payment method**. Compare the saved **DEFAULT** marker on the
intended masked card. This affects software billing, not customer payment cards.

## Delete a non-default subscription card

Operator opens the intended non-default card's menu > **Delete**. To remove the
current default, first make another saved card the default as a separately
reviewed choice. Reopen the list to confirm intended removal and retained default;
do not delete another method to clear a billing error.

## Diagnose a failed subscription renewal

For a failed subscription, operator checks expiry/details, insufficient funds
and issuer blocks privately, then corrects the method, asks the issuer or chooses
another eligible card. Do not repeatedly retry a charge. Persistent errors go to
portal **?** Support with minimum necessary
non-sensitive information; never full card/CVC. An unpaid invoice can also pause
payouts. Compare actual renewal/invoice state after operator correction; a saved
card alone proves neither paid renewal nor the end of a separate Payments review.

## Dispute preparation and status

The card-issuing bank decides dispute outcomes, not Moovs. Operator checks
**Finances > Disputes** privately for the intended case, status and actual due date.
The status article says a merchant loss marks the payment failed; that is not
permission to charge again. Do not promise success, a fixed review duration or
reversal of the bank's ruling.

The older chargeback guide distinguishes a chargeback's disputed-amount/fee debit
from an inquiry without that initial debit. It describes next-payout deduction,
a possible bank debit if funds are insufficient, and issuer provisional credit.
Full/partial wins and losses can return different amounts/fees. Explain these as
dated process descriptions, not a current fee quote, guaranteed debit or refund;
the operator checks the actual case/provider privately. A customer's provisional
bank credit is not a separate merchant refund.

For a booking-related evidence checklist, explain the public route **reservation >
More > View PDFs** for signed Terms & Conditions and Price Summary. Dispatch logs
can support service history. Gather only authorized relevant documents in the
approved private destination and review completeness. Keep raw identity/card
information hidden; evidence submission requires exact current case/content approval.
A log is evidence to evaluate, not conclusive proof.

A chargeback and a refund can both return funds. **Do not issue or recommend a
separate refund while a dispute is open.** If closed and refund is unavailable,
Support should verify the state before any other reimbursement. Do not advise
sending money outside Moovs or accepting a dispute as a troubleshooting shortcut.

### Relevant private evidence

Use the actual dispute reason, not a generic demand for every document:

- Fraudulent/unrecognized: relevant authorization, service timing and any
  acknowledgement of the charge; a reason label does not prove fraud.
- Duplicate: whether the charges concern distinct services, their receipts and
  explanation/communications; do not automatically accept or refund from a label.
- Subscription canceled: relevant cancellation timing, disclosed policy and
  notifications. This reason is not the operator's Moovs software renewal.
- Service not received/unacceptable: promised versus provided service, timing,
  dispatch/service history and relevant complaint, cancellation or remedy records.
- Credit not processed: refund chronology, applicable disclosed policy and
  related communications, without deciding legal entitlement or refunding again.
- General: relevant authorization, service and prior-resolution evidence.

Review only permitted, relevant documents in the approved private workflow. Do not
collect identity/card/IP details or broad documents in chat; redact unnecessary
identifiers. Customer/bank contact and evidence submission require exact current
recipient/case/content approval, not merely a request to explain a dispute. Source retail shipping/digital-order examples do not establish
Moovs booking fields or required evidence. The booking-specific PDF/log checklist
above is the documented Moovs starting point, not proof of a winning claim.

### Submission finality and conflicting public prose

The older guide describes available data being populated and sent at the due
date, additions before that date and no further files after submission. It names
**Submit response**, but current case/provider instructions and secure controls
must be confirmed before exact current case/content approval and submission. Do not promise automatic
submission, assume inactivity means nothing will be sent or promise a later
supplement. A claimed customer agreement does not close the case: the source says
the customer asks their bank to withdraw it and the merchant still supplies
relevant agreement evidence. This is explanation, not permission to contact them.
For a non-Moovs payment provider, use its supported response process privately.

The same article also says no evidence accepts a chargeback, suggests a full
inquiry refund can prevent chargeback, and describes reopening a paused store
by buying a plan. These conflict with its automatic-response/final no-open-dispute
refund warning or lack a documented Moovs booking procedure. Do not advise silence,
an inquiry refund, emailed-link login, a plan purchase, store reopening or imported
manual-capture/fraud-analysis controls. Ask Support/the actual provider to clarify
the case; retain the no-separate-refund boundary above.

## Marketing and sensitive-evidence boundaries

Reviewed 2026-10-16. The [May 20, 2026 comparison post](https://www.moovsapp.com/post/limo-anywhere-alternative)
uses zero-tolerance/incident-free payment and no-third-party-sharing language.
These are not a guarantee of no chargebacks, fraud losses or external processing.
Follow the specific payment-risk/dispute guidance and the issuer's actual case.

The [December 4, 2024 fraud post](https://www.moovsapp.com/post/how-to-avoid-chargebacks-in-the-transportation-industry)
suggests matching card and identity. Do not collect, copy or paste identity
or card images into AI, chat or public artifacts to follow that advice. The
operator handles necessary evidence privately through the approved secure
workflow. Neither an authorization nor collected evidence guarantees a win.
