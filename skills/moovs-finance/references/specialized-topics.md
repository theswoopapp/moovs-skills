current add/save controls or a confirmed linking result. Do not invent them.

## Review contractual Capital minimum

Dated manual guidance; current supported controls must match. The dated FAQ
points to the **external Stripe Dashboard > Capital** tab or loan agreement for
the minimum. Operator privately reviews their own agreement, current payment
period and credited repayments; no amounts, agreement files or account details
need to enter chat. Inspect only authorized non-sensitive contract/status values;
do not expose raw identifiers or calculate a legal/financial obligation as advice.

The FAQ describes percentage-of-sales withholding, credit toward the period's
minimum and an automatic bank debit for a remaining shortfall. Actual contract
and current state govern; do not infer debit date/amount, universal loan term,
that a debit occurred or that the minimum is met. It also says withholding can
continue after the minimum, repayments cover past-due before current amounts,
and excess does not prepay future minimums. These are dated explanations, not
instructions to move funds. Difficulty meeting an obligation goes to the supported
lender channel; no promised waiver, extension or advice to pay early.

## Additional or full Capital repayment

Reviewed 2026-10-16. Apply task-authority.md. The public FAQ expressly permits
additional payments or full repayment through **external Stripe Dashboard >
Capital > Make Payment**. This is repayment of an existing financing record,
not Moovs subscription billing, a customer charge or a new financing application.

1. Reuse the operator's explicit additional-payment or full-repayment goal. The
   authorized borrower privately confirms the correct company, existing financing
   and current agreement, and that this voluntary payment is available through
   their supported access. An offer notice alone is not an active balance.
   Do not create/connect a personal Stripe account or assume a Moovs-native tab.
2. Open only the authorized actual financing through its established
   lender access. Where the documented Dashboard route matches, use **Capital >
   Make Payment** for that record, only when ready to make the intended payment;
   do not click as a test or assume a harmless preview. If access or the action
   differs, obtain the lender's current supported instructions rather than guess.
3. Review the actual secure payment flow within approved scope, including the
   operator-authorized amount (additional or lender's current full payoff),
   financing destination, any offered payment source, disclosures and intended effect.
   Do not calculate payoff from an old balance or presume a funding source,
   field layout, final button or separate confirmation screen. If the actual
   flow cannot be understood, stop before submission and ask the lender.
4. After exact current financing, amount/currency, payment-source and terms/effect
   approval, submit the indicated payment action once in execution mode. Pause
   inspection for raw bank/card entry or personal signatures and return only to a
   non-sensitive result. No sensitive screenshots, agreement files or bank details
   in chat.
5. Return to the same financing's authorized servicing record and check
   the exact attempt's payment status/history/progress against the intended payment
   and current balance. Report observed non-sensitive status; private/unobserved
   checks are **operator-reported**, not independently verified. A pending attempt is not a completed
   repayment; full payoff needs that financing's servicing record to show it repaid,
   not just a submission message. While pending, monitor supported servicing status
   and do not resubmit. If status is missing, failed or unclear, resolve it with the
   lender before another attempt; do not pay again to test.

This is a conditional task path, not a filmed current
payment form or live repayment verification. No recommendation to repay, promised
fee reduction/new offer, or assumed stop to withholding/automatic debits follows.
Current agreement and lender servicing govern unresolved terms and status.

## 1099-K questions

Explain only that the published guide distinguishes gross transaction reporting
from take-home revenue; fees, refunds and chargebacks can make comparisons differ.
Keep raw tax/identity identifiers hidden; inspect only authorized redacted records.
Do not quote historical reporting thresholds as current law, determine whether
a form is required, or advise deductions/tax
classification. Current tax year, jurisdiction and rules require authoritative
current guidance and a qualified tax professional. Missing/incorrect form access
is an account-specific Support question, not proof of zero reportable activity.
Neutral record organization can include privately retaining transaction/form
copies, tracking fees separately and keeping tax information current. Multiple
platforms may issue separate forms; do not turn recordkeeping into tax treatment
advice or invent dashboard download/update controls.
