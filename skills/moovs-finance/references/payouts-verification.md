# Payout status and verification

Reviewed 2026-10-16. Follow task-authority.md for scoped financial readback/actions;
sensitive entry remains private with its owner.

## Standard payout questions

Have the operator open **Finances > Payouts** and privately review the intended
payout's date, amount, transfer details and status. The schedule article also
points to captured transactions awaiting payout in Finances; capture is not payout.
The current timing guide describes a rolling two-business-day schedule, excluding
weekends/holidays; first payouts, reviews and account conditions can take longer.
The schedule article mentions a seven-day basis in some countries; the banking
article mentions two-to-three-day deposits. Do not turn these different source
statements into a universal bank-posting deadline or invent the affected countries.

**In Transit** means moving to the bank; **Paid** means sent, not posted by the bank.
If Paid fewer than five business days ago, the timing guide allows further bank
time. It routes more-than-five-day cases to a private connected-bank check, then
the bank using payout date, exact amount and displayed payor, then portal **?**
Support if unlocated. It does not specify the exactly-five-day boundary. Keep
locator details between operator/bank/Support, not in chat; never promise arrival.

If no payout exists and funds await payout, Restricted/Restricted Soon/Pending
routes to Paused or restricted below; if no restriction/review is shown, use
portal **?** Support. Missing records are not permission to create a test payout.

Sources: [Payout timing](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6634448-when-will-i-receive-a-moovs-payments-payout),
[Schedule](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/13289570-how-can-i-view-my-payout-schedule-in-the-moovs-operator-app)

## Paused or restricted

Ask only which status the operator sees under **Settings > General > Payments**.

- Restricted Soon: requested information has an upcoming deadline.
- Restricted: one or more payment/payout capabilities are unavailable.
- Pending/review: no repeated submission unless there is a specific written
  request or correction; there is no guaranteed completion/release date.
- Enabled/Complete: currently in good standing, not immunity to future requests.

The operator uses **Complete Account Setup** and supplies requested verification
privately through the approved secure workflow, then returns to check outstanding
actions. Sales, CS and Support cannot remove a Payments review or promise its end.
For Restricted/Restricted Soon/Pending, unavailable setup or an active review,
use the published written Payments-team route, payments@moovsapp.com. Draft only;
no identity documents or full financial identifiers in email/chat. Other billing,
bank troubleshooting and timing issues go to portal **?** Support.

An unpaid subscription invoice can pause standard payouts and make instant
payouts unavailable. Operator privately reviews invoices and the payment method
under **Settings > Billing & Plans**, then resolves the outstanding invoice.
The guide describes automatic next-cycle resumption, not immediate bank receipt.
Its suggestion to refresh or initiate another payout is not diagnostic-transfer
permission: operator can recheck status privately, but a transfer is a separately
requested task. Do not declare recovery from invoice/card status alone; remaining
restrictions follow the status-specific route.

Sources: [Paused payouts](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/16257302-why-are-my-payouts-paused),
[Restricted Soon](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/9386632-why-am-i-seeing-restricted-soon-in-my-moovs-payments)

## Bank changes and instant payouts

These are payout destinations/transfers, not subscription cards or customer
charges. Use only the intended approved task below; private bank entry remains
with its owner, while ordinary non-sensitive UI actions can be executed.

## Add a payout bank account

**Settings > General > Payments > Add Bank Account**, then **Complete account
setup** with required fields before saving. Operator supplies bank details
privately; never collect or validate routing/account numbers. Reopen the intended
masked connected account/status without initiating a test payout.

## Choose the default payout bank for a currency

On the intended account under Payments, operator opens its three-dot menu >
**Make Default for Currency**. Explain that this changes where payouts go.
Read back the intended currency/default and masked destination privately; a
newly added bank or saved default does not prove funds have arrived there.

## Initiate an eligible instant payout

Instant-payout documentation discusses account eligibility, limits and fees.
It describes a rolling reset 24 hours after the last instant transfer, not midnight,
and historical usage/extra-processor fees. Check current account terms; do not
universalize its default limit, reset, fee or bank list. Have the operator
confirm current eligible balance, destination, fee and limit themselves before
deciding whether to submit. Public route: **Settings > General > Payments >
Start Instant Payout**. This action is documented as initiating the transfer;
do not click merely to inspect or assume a later confirmation screen. Establish
exact current amount/currency, masked destination, fee/terms and effect approval
before executing it once. Read the actual
payout result/status before any retry; initiation is not bank-posting proof.

## Request review of an instant payout limit

The guide routes an increase request to in-app Support chat or email; the
Payments team reviews account history, subscription standing and payout activity.
Offer an unsent minimal request stating the desired review, without financial
identifiers/documents. No guaranteed increase or minimum approval; use the
decision and actual updated limit, not request submission, as the result.

## Manual payout request at the instant limit

The public instant-payout guide mentions requesting a manual payout after hitting
the limit, with additional verification and a longer process. It does not establish
a request route, exact controls or current approval criteria; its article body
has no embedded image/video procedure. Treat this as a documentation gap. Ask
Moovs Support for the approved process before an operator request; do not invent
a payout button, bypass a limit or promise approval/timing from this mention.
[Public overview](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8182121-understanding-instant-payouts)

## Batching is not bank receipt

The older batching article says Moovs handles batching automatically: transactions
before 4 PM Pacific join that day's group; after that cutoff they join the next.
Holiday/weekend and bank-processing effects can delay deposits. This is dated
grouping guidance, not a manual settle instruction or account-specific payout
promise. Check only the authorized current payout record instead.

Source: [Batching](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6827604-when-are-my-moovs-payments-transactions-batched-or-settled)
