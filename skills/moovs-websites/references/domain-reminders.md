# Dated domain-expiration reminder offer

Reviewed 2026-10-16; supporting service-intake guidance, not a registrar workflow.
The [December 4, 2024 Moovs post](https://www.moovsapp.com/post/prevent-your-domain-from-expiring-before-its-too-late)
offers a free service sending reminders one month and one week before domain
expiry and directs readers to a form. Its public HTML embeds a client-rendered
HubSpot form. The form is not proven absent: runtime fields, availability and
submission were not inspected, and nothing was submitted.

Ask whether the operator wants to understand the offer, check existing reminder
arrangements or inquire about enrollment. The dated offer does not establish
that the service remains available or that this domain is enrolled. Confirm
current service terms and supported intake with Moovs before any submission;
do not invent required fields, a Moovs Settings toggle or an enrollment status.
The operator reviews the actual form/destination and enters only agreed data
privately. Never collect registrar credentials, codes or payment details.

The post links an external [domain-expiry lookup](https://whois.domaintools.com/).
That is a separate service, not proof of authoritative expiry or permission to
query an operator's domain. The domain owner should check renewal/expiry in their
registrar account privately. Reminder service is not domain renewal, auto-renew,
DNS editing or a guarantee that a domain will never expire. Do not purchase,
renew, change DNS or submit a domain as part of explaining this offer.

If the operator later submits under separately confirmed instructions, describe
only the observed confirmation; an intake acknowledgement is not proof of active
reminders, delivery at both intervals or successful renewal. Avoid duplicate
submission on an uncertain result and ask Moovs to confirm the existing request.
