# Plan comparison and subscription changes

Reviewed 2026-10-16 from public guides. Follow task-authority.md: exact reviewed
cost/term/effect approval is required for checkout, plan changes or purchases.
Sensitive payment entry and personal signatures remain private with their owner.

## Compare or upgrade

**Settings > Billing and Plans** shows current subscription and available plan
information. Public guidance uses **Upgrade Plan** for comparison/upgrade steps.
Explain the choices using current non-sensitive account information and public
feature guidance; do not click through checkout to discover prices. Avoid billing
cards, credentials and sensitive invoices. A visible price/feature card is not
proof that payment succeeded or access was enabled.

For HOW requests, coach the steps; for an explicit purchase task, select the
intended plan and review actual costs/terms under task authority. The owner
completes sensitive payment entry privately; approved non-sensitive checkout
steps can be executed under exact current purchase approval.
Explaining this step is not authorization for an assistant purchase, nor a reason
to require checkout for a comparison-only question. No exact checkout button is
established by the public text; do not invent one.

After an operator-authorized purchase, read back the resulting plan and relevant
feature access, not just a toast. If status is unknown, do not submit again.
[Upgrade/compare](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/9706969-how-to-upgrade-your-moovs-subscription),
[Paid-plan features](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/13294451-how-do-i-upgrade-to-a-paid-plan-in-the-moovs-operator-app-and-what-features-are-included).

## Setup fees and returning from Free

The [setup-fee article](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/9709578-what-is-the-setup-fee-why-do-i-need-to-pay-for-it)
lists **$299** when moving from Free to Standard, Pro or Enterprise and warns
that returning from Free after a downgrade incurs the fee again. This is a
published amount, not the account's final price or a fee-waiver promise. Current
[pricing](https://www.moovsapp.com/pricing) instead says **Get in touch** for
Enterprise setup; disclose that conflict rather than promising $299 for every
Enterprise contract. Do not extend the Free/re-upgrade example to every
paid-to-paid tier change or assume access was purchased.
Have the operator verify current terms and ask Moovs about ambiguity before
purchase. Never trigger the fee as a troubleshooting step.

## Downgrade or pause

The public upgrade guide directs downgrade requests to Moovs Chat. The
[pause-policy guide](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8102622-can-i-pause-my-subscription-exploring-flexibility-and-alternatives)
states there is no formal self-serve subscription-pause option, while describing
Support-led downgrade/case-by-case alternatives. Explain these as requests, not
guaranteed suspension, refund, cancellation or continued feature access.

Help prepare a reviewable Support inquiry with the desired effective date,
operational needs and specific questions. Operator sends it. Do not invent a
Pause button, cancel the plan, delete records to lower usage or claim a requested
change is already applied. Only actual account readback/support confirmation
can establish the outcome and terms.
