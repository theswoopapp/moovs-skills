# Customer trip page and communication boundaries

Reviewed 2026-10-16 against public Help prose, not a current signed-in screen or
verified message delivery. Use this only for the intended reservation/trip and
recipient. Customer links, phone numbers and location details stay private.
Operator/customer performs every setting change, field selection, save, call and
send in their own identity; do not contact anyone or start a trip as a test.

## Help a customer review their trip page

1. The intended customer opens the customer trip-page link from their own trip
   status text. Do not request the link or log in as them. Confirm the intended
   service date/trip privately; a copied or opened link does not prove delivery.
2. They can review trip/driver status and passenger, trip, stop and vehicle
   details. For a map, expand **Driver Tracking** and view the map. Tracking
   depends on dispatch status and location availability. A trip page/map link does
   not prove live location is available; a missing position is not by itself a
   failure. Do not start/advance a real trip to force tracking or equate a map
   with driver acceptance.
3. The **Driver Contact** card offers call/text. The customer decides whether
   contact is wanted and privately initiates it; opening the card is not a call,
   sent text or evidence of driver response. No driver phone/location copied here.
4. A completed-trip text may include a review link **if review collection is
   enabled**. This is not a review-collection setup procedure or proof of review.

## Explain recipients without overwriting passenger identity

Public Help locates SMS controls at **Settings > General > Communication**, then
**Chat > Contacts**, after Chat is enabled for the account. Automated trip texts
come from the company's Moovs Chat number. Reminder/status recipients can be
Passenger, Booking Contact, both or neither; reminders are described at 24 hours
and 1 hour before pickup. Status updates depend on configured Driver/Member
triggers and may have trip-specific recipient settings. Check the actual offered
controls and existing delivery settings before any separately requested human edit.
Do not enable Chat, change global defaults or send to diagnose one missing text.

The older passenger-role article suggests replacing passenger phone with the
booker's to redirect updates. Do not overwrite identity data as that workaround;
review supported recipient settings instead. Same-phone deduplication can apply.
**STOP** blocks all SMS from that number, including reminders/status texts, until
the contact themselves chooses to reply **START**; never bypass their opt-out.

The broad trip-communication article says creation emails Booking Contact, but
operator creation Help distinguishes **Save** from **Save and Send Email**.
Retain that action-specific choice; do not promise all saves email or that save-only
suppresses every other notification. Logged sending is not inbox delivery.
