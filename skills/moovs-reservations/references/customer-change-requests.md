# Review a customer's pending trip-change request

Reviewed 2026-10-16 from literal February/March 2025 product recordings and current
public Portal settings prose. Dated controls must match the intended company's
current UI. This is an existing reservation's **change request**, not an
unconfirmed reservation request, cancellation, quote conversion or a new trip.

Confirm company, reservation and exact trip, customer's requested outcome and
operator permission to decide it. Customer and operator act in their own identity;
never submit a customer change as a test. Approved settings, fields and decision actions follow
[task authority](task-authority.md), not blanket human-click handoffs. Without authorized browser inspection, guide manually
and call readback operator-reported. Keep private trip links and identities out
of chat. Give the next step and wait, rather than silently applying changes.

## Compare, decide and verify the changed trip

1. Open **Reservations** and locate the intended request. March's list shows a
   blue indicator on reservations with pending changes. Open the exact reservation
   and trip; do not interpret a blue dot alone as a complete change description.
2. Confirm the banner **The customer has requested changes to this trip** and
   compare the current/proposed itinerary. **View original trip** switches to the
   original details; **View proposed trip** returns to the requested version.
   Review each changed pickup/end date/time, mapped address and stop order, plus
   the unchanged intended contact/service. A proposed view is not saved acceptance.
3. Have the operator decide feasibility: intended service, vehicle/driver timing,
   conflicts, existing commitments and any price/customer impact. Do not assume
   the quoted fare automatically recalculates or that existing assignments remain
   suitable. Resolve a needed pricing/assignment decision separately, not by
   altering global settings to make the requested itinerary fit.
4. For the approved exact proposal, the authorized actor selects **Apply** once.
   The recording shows immediate processing, not a second confirmation dialog.
   Narration says an automatic customer email is sent; explain that consequence
   before submission. Do not manually re-key the proposal or send again to test.
5. Reopen the same saved trip and compare its actual endpoint dates/times,
   addresses/stop order and affected details with the approved proposal. The
   recorded result loses the pending-change banner and shows updated trip details;
   read those values, not just the success toast. Review unchanged trips and any
   actual price/status effects. Unknown outcome means inspect before retry.

**Decline** is visible, but the inspected sequence applies rather than declines.
Do not invent a decline confirmation, reason requirement, resulting state or
notification contract. Before an intended decline, reconcile its actual current
controls/effects with supported guidance; operator owns the decision and submission.
A cancellation request is separate and is not processed by this Apply walkthrough.

## Why some requests need review

Current [Portal Settings](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6365893-customer-portal-settings)
places **Automate trip updates requested by customer** at **Settings > Customer
Portal > Settings** with its own Hours/Days cutoff. Before that configured cutoff,
submitted changes can apply automatically. March demonstrates a within-cutoff
request waiting for operator decision. Read existing settings for explanation;
do not change this company-wide policy to resolve one pending request.

shows list indicator, original/proposed switch, Apply/Decline, processing and the
