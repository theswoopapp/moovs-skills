# General reservation flight tracking

Reviewed 2026-10-15. [Pricing](https://www.moovsapp.com/pricing) names FlightAware
flight tracking. This is not a setup/matching/status procedure, and a Shuttle
flight field or driver notification setting does not establish the general
reservation workflow. No private airline/customer record is needed for coaching.

## FlightAware matching and trip effects

For the now-supported new arrival-airport booking sequence, use [arrival-flight creation](arrival-flight-creation.md). Its public creation GIF demonstrates matching and saved flight-card association, not the departure/existing-trip/freshness/disruption contract discussed here.

Confirm the intended reservation/trip, service date/timezone, airport and whether
the question is flight identification, status freshness, pickup timing or a
notification. Use only the minimum authorized record; flight numbers without
service dates can be ambiguous. Check actual product access and visible state.

The [driver-notification article](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8002457-driver-notifications-send-driver-a-text-if-flight-time-is-updated)
describes a text when an airport-pickup flight arrival changes by at least 15
minutes. It does not establish exact general flight entry/matching controls,
coverage, refresh timing, cancellation/diversion handling, trip repricing or all
automatic pickup/assignment/customer-message effects. Do not infer those effects
or treat a status/notification as the driver's acceptance of a new pickup time.

July's narration explains an arrival-pickup offset: if pickup is intentionally
later than flight arrival, the described early/late flight update preserves that
chosen interval. Its 15-20 minutes is an example, not a required/default buffer.
For an already tracked airport pickup, compare the intended flight/date, arrival
and pickup time in the same timezone and the chosen interval; after an actual
update, check whether that interval is still shown. Do not change flight/trip
times to test it or promise unconditional automatic updates. Exact offset entry,
matching/update constraints and cancellation/diversion effects remain unresolved;
a notification threshold is separate and not driver acceptance of a changed time.
[July 2026, 45:27-46:59](https://fast.wistia.com/embed/iframe/64e8h1tobn?wtime=2727s).

Read a current public guide for the actual task. If none establishes the required
action, disclose the gap and obtain supported Moovs instructions before directing
flight/time edits. Do not substitute FlightAware's separate consumer/API account
setup, ask for a key, buy tracking or reschedule a real trip as a test.
All edits remain operator-performed and can affect service or communications.
For a supported intended change, compare the saved flight/date, displayed status
and freshness, trip time and separately any notification history. Without direct
authorized readback, report operator-reported state, not verified live tracking.
