# Charter and multiday itinerary coaching

Reviewed 2026-10-16 using public March/April/May 2026 webinar demonstrations.

## Scope, access and handoff

Confirm company, existing versus new booking, all service/idle days, vehicle,
operational requirements and operator-approved pricing inputs. March's demo
describes Enterprise access; May describes a Motor Coach/Charter add-on. Check
actual purchased entitlement and enablement rather than treating either dated
description as proof that a plan alone unlocks Charter.

The operator performs every field/dropdown edit, calculation application, day
addition, save, send, driver assignment/unassignment and charge. These are not
read-only previews. Explain the affected scope before each action. Use authorized
browser tools only; otherwise guide manually and label outcomes operator-reported.
Never collect credentials, customer payment details or unrelated passenger data.

## Create and read back a Single Day Charter

This is a bounded March 2026 test-environment recording, not current-account
validation. Use it only when the visible Charter flow matches; stop on differences.
That recorded submission is Single Day only; the complete operator-owned
add-days route below is distinct from filmed extra-day submission evidence.

1. From **Reservations > Create**, operator selects the intended booking contact,
   **Charter > Single Day**, then **Charter Date**. Confirm the service date.
2. Operator opens **Add Vehicle**, selects the intended vehicle, then confirms
   **Add Vehicle** in its dialog. Review **Garage Addresses > Depart From / Return
   To** against the intended operation; do not accept defaults without checking.
   Selecting a vehicle does not prove availability or driver-hours feasibility.
3. In **Day 1**, operator enters **First Stop** address and **Pick-up Time**, uses
   **+ Add Stop** for agreed intermediate stops, and enters **Last Stop** address
   and **Drop-off Time**. Select complete addresses from suggestions and review
   their order. One intermediate time is blank in the demo; this does not make all
   stop/time fields optional. Follow current validation. Do not use **As Directed**
   as an undocumented bypass. Review any **Day Note** needed for the itinerary.
4. Operator uses **Calculate Distance & Time**. Compare **Trip Distance**, **Trip
   Time**, deadhead and **Charter Summary**. The recording shows manual distance
   and hours/minutes adjustments; use only operator-approved operational inputs,
   never its example buffers. Estimates do not establish safe or legal scheduling.
5. Operator uses **Calculate Price**, then inspects **BRA Calculations**. Review
   service time/distance, departure/return deadhead, pricing adjustments and the
   **Total Amount** against approved rates. Close the breakdown and review the
   one-day itinerary before submission. This is not pricing-configuration coaching.
6. The recorded Charter submit button is **Create Reservation**, not a demonstrated
   Save/Send selector. The authorized actor submits after reviewing current action
   and notification constraints. General public confirmation Help
   distinguishes **Save reservation** from **Save reservation & send email**: the
   latter sends the booking-contact confirmation; the former does not. Those labels
   do not establish what this Charter button sends. Inspect the current submission
   and recipient options; if a required no-send outcome is unclear, pause before
   submitting and obtain a supported procedure. Do not send or charge as a test.
7. The recording shows a saved-charter notice with **View Reservation**, followed
   by the saved **Charter Itinerary** and day details. Open the intended saved record
   and compare reservation/day references, one-day count, date, both endpoint
   times, ordered stops, vehicle, trip/deadhead totals, price and status. A notice
   alone is insufficient. Keep saved state, notifications, dispatch and payment
   separate; unknown outcome means inspect before retry, never create a duplicate.

## Build and review the documented itinerary

For a simple consecutive-service-day itinerary, the March recording explicitly
shows that days can be added to an existing Charter. It does not show a completed
extra-day submission. The complete operator-owned route below uses that documented
action and the actual current form, not an invented final button or a claim that
the dated preview was saved. Use the existing Charter when one already exists.

For a genuinely new booking, the supported first-day creation above followed by
**Add Day** can build the agreed multiday itinerary. Do not create separate
reservations for the remaining days or duplicate a booking merely to follow this
route. This is not a demonstrated initial **Multi-Day** submission procedure.
The recording shows **Multi-Day**, **Start Date** and **End Date**, but switches
to Single Day before entering the itinerary. If the operator specifically needs
that initial form rather than adding days, inspect current public guidance/current
form and disclose the remaining direct-creation gap; do not pretend the whole
multiday form was filmed.

## Complete an additional Charter day and read back the whole itinerary

1. Open the intended existing reservation and its **Charter Itinerary**. Compare
   the saved day references/dates with the approved plan and identify the exact
   next service day. Do not assume an extra day is absent from an unknown prior
   attempt; inspect first. For a new booking, finish and read back the first day
   using the Single Day procedure above before continuing on that same record.
2. Operator selects **Add Day**. Check that the drawer targets that reservation
   and set **Date** to the approved day. A suggested next date or copied prior
   endpoint is a convenience, not evidence it matches the customer's plan.
3. Operator completes **First Stop** address and **Pick-up Time**, the ordered
   intermediate addresses/times using **+ Add Stop**, **Last Stop** address and
   **Drop-off Time**, and any required **Day Note**. Select complete address
   suggestions and check local dates/AM/PM. Follow current required fields; neither
   a blank field in the demo nor **As Directed** is a validation bypass.
4. Operator uses the visible **Calculate Distance & Time** and reviews **Trip
   Distance**, **Trip Time**, the selected **Vehicle** and **Charter Summary**.
   Compare the day's operation and the whole itinerary against approved timing,
   vehicle availability and pricing. Review any current price calculation and
   resulting day/charter totals before accepting them; the Add Day footage does
   not establish a particular price button, automatic repricing rule or unchanged
   return deadhead. Do not reuse the demonstration's prices or scheduling buffers.
5. Operator finishes the actual current Add Day form and its required validation,
   then personally performs its clearly identified day-add submission after
   reviewing the complete change and price. The recording establishes the
   **Add Day** action but not its final submission label: do not invent **Save**,
   **Add** or a silent-save promise. If the current form's submission action,
   target or required notification constraints cannot be understood, pause there
   for supported clarification rather than guessing. Do not treat closing the
   drawer, a preview or a success notice as saved-day proof.
6. Reopen the same reservation and the newly added day. Compare the new saved
   day/reference, exact date, both endpoint times, ordered stops, vehicle and
   distance/time/pricing against the approved plan. Review existing days too,
   including their dates, vehicle and revised charter totals/deadhead; report any
   unexpected change before continuing. Without browser access this is the
   operator's readback, not independently verified success. If the result is
   unknown, inspect current state before retrying; never add the day twice.
7. Repeat on that same Charter only for the remaining agreed consecutive service
   days, checking saved state after each addition. Finish by opening every day
   and comparing the complete intended date set/day count, ordered daily
   itineraries, vehicle and charter total with the approved worksheet. A correct
   first day or one completed drawer is not the requested full itinerary outcome.

Keep completion of the itinerary separate from confirmation delivery, charging,
dispatch and driver assignment. No additional send, charge or dispatch is part of
this procedure. March describes Enterprise access; May describes a Motor
Coach/Charter add-on, so actual purchased entitlement/enablement and role still
matter, not a guessed universal plan matrix. If a required no-notification outcome
cannot be established for the current action, pause before submitting.

This simple route does not establish initial Multi-Day form submission, idle-day
or nonconsecutive-day rules, duplicate-date handling, day removal/reordering,
relay drivers or pricing configuration. Those variants require supported guidance
when actually requested; their missing matrices do not by themselves block a
straightforward consecutive-service-day addition that the current form supports.

The Add Day drawer is visibly closed without saving in this demonstration; the

## Single-day Charter crossing midnight

The April demonstration keeps an overnight itinerary within one Charter day,
with the final stop explicitly dated the **next calendar day**. Review both dates,
AM/PM and ordered stop times before the operator enters or changes them; do not
silently put an early-morning endpoint on the pickup date or split the charter
merely because it crosses midnight.

The demonstrated endpoint must be **before noon the following day**, with 11:59 AM
named as the latest time, not noon or an unrestricted overnight duration. This is
a dated product demonstration, not a guarantee that every current configuration
accepts it. Inspect current validation. If the agreed itinerary reaches noon or
later, pause for a supported scheduling decision rather than shortening the trip
or inventing a workaround. Operator calculates/reviews distance and price, then
saves only after all dates are correct. Reopen and read back the endpoint's date
and time as well as the start; a correct time alone is insufficient.

## Charter-wide driver changes

The May demonstration shows assigning a driver applies across the charter's
days; unassigning removes that driver across the charter. Review the **whole**
itinerary and driver suitability before operator selection, not just one visible
day. Do not promise independent day-by-day acceptance or treat assignment as
proof of driver acceptance, availability or legal qualification.

## Verify without accidental duplicates

After operator submission, reopen the intended itinerary and compare all dates,
stops, timing, vehicle, pricing and changed assignments. Inspect multiple days
when a charter-wide assignment changed. Keep saved state, sent notifications,
driver acceptance and collected money separate. Unknown outcome: inspect current
state before repeating. No real charge, send or dispatch as a test.

The inspected narration/frames do not establish every Charter form field, initial
Multi-Day submission, idle-day validation, pricing configuration or driver relay.
The add-days route requires the operator to complete the actual current form; it
is not a current-tenant acceptance claim or an invented final click sequence.
