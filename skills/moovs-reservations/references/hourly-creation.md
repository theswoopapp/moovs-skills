# Create an Hourly reservation

Reviewed 2026-10-16. The literal Help GIF has an April2023 filename and visible
trip date; its actual recording date is unverified. It is not current authenticated
UI. Use these details only when controls match; company/contact, task-scoped editing,
pricing and deliberate save/email checks still apply. This is an Hourly
**reservation**, not initial Hourly quote creation or a Charter itinerary.

1. Confirm a new booking is intended and no existing matching reservation needs
   editing instead. **Reservations > Create** opens **Create New Reservation**.
   Operator selects the intended booking contact, separate passenger if needed,
   order type and **Hourly** Trip Type. Do not copy demonstration records.
2. Operator enters pickup date/time and selects mapped pickup, intermediate-stop
   and final drop-off addresses from actual suggestions; review their order.
   **Add Stop** adds an intermediate stop. When that stop needs arrival/count/note
   details, its edit control opens **Additional Stop 1 Info** (number varies).
   Operator reviews the date/time, passenger count/note and chooses its **Save**.
   This saves stop details, not the whole reservation.
3. Final drop-off's edit control opens **Additional Drop-off Info**. Operator sets
   the intended **Drop-off Date & Time**, reviews date/AM-PM/chronology and any
   needed drop-off count/note, then that dialog's **Save**. An Hourly end time is
   not established merely by typing a price or billed-hours value.
4. Operator chooses the intended **Add Vehicle**, then reviews approved hourly
   rate/billed hours, other price items and total. Demonstration rates/fees are
   not Moovs defaults or business policy. Check the intended service duration
   and pricing separately; vehicle selection does not prove availability.
5. Inspect the final save choice. The GIF demonstrates **Save reservation**,
   a saving state, a success notice/**View Reservation**, then a saved Hourly
   trip—not a charge, dispatch or observed confirmation-email delivery. Public
   confirmation help separately distinguishes **Save reservation** from **Save
   reservation & send email**. Operator deliberately selects/submits the intended
   offered action once; do not promise every other notification is suppressed.
6. Open the saved record and compare its reference/contact/passenger, Hourly type,
   exact pickup/end dates/times and duration, ordered addresses/stop details,
   vehicle and agreed pricing. Review actual payment status without changing it.
   A saved-stop dialog/toast alone is not this readback. If final save is uncertain,
   inspect existing state before retrying to avoid a duplicate. Without authorized
   direct inspection, completion is operator-reported.
