# Review an approved cancellation fee before canceling

Reviewed 2026-10-16. Current public cancellation/payment/refund article bodies
and July2025 narrated training support the bounded pre-cancellation sequence
below. Captions are historical narration, not current UI or a successful payment
attestation. This is not legal advice about whether a fee is enforceable.

## Separate the requested actions

Confirm company, exact reservation/trip, intended cancellation and the operator's
already approved fee/policy. Do not decide a fee or collect money merely because
the operator asked to cancel. Price adjustment, collection, cancellation, refund
and customer notification are separate human-owned actions; a saved policy does
not execute them. Existing payment review is useful even when no charge is wanted.

Check actual plan, payment access and role before collection. Free does not
include Stripe/card processing or payment links, and cannot record external-method
payments as Paid. A fee or old error message grants no payment workaround.
The operator enters sensitive payment information privately; do not inspect it.

## Pre-cancellation review and handoff

1. On the intended trip, review its current total, successful payments, prior
   refunds, pending/unclear attempts and Amount Due privately with the operator.
   Keep any other trips' payments separate. Match the agreed retained amount
   against actual records; a fee target is not necessarily another full charge.
2. Explain the July2025 narration: revise the intended total to the agreed fee,
   collect only an approved unpaid remainder if needed, then cancel the trip.
   The presenter corrects a total-versus-remainder example; never copy demo
   amounts or assume every ledger follows an inferred subtraction formula.
   Before changing price or charging, resolve any mismatch with current displayed
   state and supported Moovs guidance. Already retained funds are not charged again.
3. Only for an explicitly requested, reconciled collection, current payment Help
   names the selected trip's **Edit Trip > Money > Charge Customer**, where an
   operator reviews custom amount, method and optional note before final charge.
   This is the trip, not **Charge Reservation** allocation across other trips.
   Operator performs approved price edits through matching current controls;
   neither the fee video nor this guide establishes a universal fee-entry field.
4. After any operator payment submission, inspect the original payment history
   and amount/status before proceeding. Pending, timeout or ambiguous outcome
   requires resolution, not another charge or a switch to an external method.
   Confirm the intended retained fee is reconciled before the operator uses
   the exact trip's **More/View More > Cancel Trip**. Cancellation alone is not
   proof of collection or a refund, and notification depends on enabled settings.
5. Reopen the reservation view, not the **Reopen** mutation, to compare this trip's
   canceled state, price summary and payment/refund history. Other trips must stay
   unchanged. The historical video describes retained paid amounts in its price
   summary; verify the actual current result rather than promise automatic labels.
   Without direct authorized non-sensitive readback, label it operator-reported.

## Already canceled, refund due, or unexplained balance

Do not turn the video's reopen/charge/recancel demonstration into a generic
recovery recipe. For an already canceled trip or unexplained paid/fee/balance
state, review existing records privately and obtain current Moovs instructions
before another price change, reopening, charge or cancellation. A Dispatch status
label is not a substitute for the documented cancellation action/readback.

Any requested refund is separate: identify the original successful payment and
remaining refundable amount; current Help uses its payment menu > Refund.
Card returns go to the original card, external records do not themselves move
money, and an open dispute must not be refunded separately. Do not perform a
refund or change notifications to make this fee workflow appear complete.
