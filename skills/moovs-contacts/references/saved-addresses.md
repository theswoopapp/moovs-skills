# Save and reuse contact home/work addresses

Reviewed 2026-10-16 from retained November 2025 public caption narration, not
literal frames or a current account. Use the contacts entrypoint's access,
task-scoped execution and private-entry boundaries. These are profile-wide frequent
addresses, not a one-trip override or a saved venue Location/Point.

## Store an intended frequent address

1. Identify the exact contact in **Contacts** and corroborate with a second
   non-sensitive identifier. Agree the intended home/work slot and location.
   Narration permits frequent destinations in these slots; do not assume the
   label establishes where someone actually lives or works.
2. Operator enters only the agreed address in the offered **Home address** or
   **Work address** field and completes its actual save/auto-save behavior.
   The captions give no exact final control or required-field sequence. Stop if
   current fields differ; do not invent extra slots from a roadmap promise.
3. Reopen that same contact and privately compare the requested address/slot.
   A profile save does not prove any booking changed or that old trips update.

## Reuse during an intended reservation

1. With the intended contact selected during creation, inspect the offered saved
   address suggestions for the pickup/drop-off. November narration identifies a
   home icon for home and a business icon for work; compare the actual address,
   not the icon alone. It does not establish a hover label or present-day layout.
2. Operator chooses only the intended stop's saved address and reviews the full
   itinerary, date/time and passenger before the reservation's normal save choice.
   Selecting an address is not a completed booking. Do not create a real test
   reservation merely to demonstrate reuse.
3. Reopen the saved intended reservation and compare the selected stop/location
   separately from the contact's profile. Unknown save outcome needs readback,
   not a duplicate booking. Without authorized direct readback, report the result
   as operator-reported.
