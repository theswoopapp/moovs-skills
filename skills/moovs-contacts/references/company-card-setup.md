# Add a card to an existing Company

Reviewed 2026-10-16 using public Help prose and dated embedded GIF frames.
Not a current tenant test. This is Company card addition, not Company card
management/deletion, passenger-card assignment or Moovs subscription billing.

## Confirm the intended setup

Confirm the authorized company/session, exact existing Company profile, intended
payer/cardholder and explicit intent to add a Company payment method. Do not create
a duplicate Company or use a person's contact merely because its name is similar.
Before initiating this Stripe-backed setup, confirm an eligible paid plan,
permissions and already configured Moovs Payments readiness. The public
[Payments Settings guide](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6383795-payments-settings)
uses **Connected** as the payment-readiness status; a paid plan alone is not enough. [Free policy](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/9927108-important-updates-for-free-plan-users)
excludes payment processing, charges and payment links. If this is Free, stop this
Stripe-backed payment-method setup; do not offer a Stripe/payment-link workaround.
A visible Add Card proves neither entitlement nor separate Free card storage.
Do not upgrade, buy, connect a processor or touch subscription billing as incidental
setup; an eligible supported path requires a separately authorized business decision.

## Company payment-method private handoff

1. In **Contacts**, open **Companies** and the intended existing Company.
   Corroborate its identity with a second relevant non-sensitive business detail.
2. Find **Credit Cards > Add Card** within that Company drawer. The public
   Company guide explicitly permits adding cards on this page; its dated GIFs
   show this Company-specific opener. If current controls differ, stop and
   reconcile current public Help/Support before entry; do not use the personal
   contact's Add Card as a substitute.
3. Pause browser inspection and screen capture before opening the Company's
   **Add Card** flow if it could reveal card information, and always before any
   sensitive entry. The authorized operator opens and privately completes the
   actual Company card-add flow, including its final submission. Do not share the
   operator session with a cardholder. Any bank authentication belongs to the
   cardholder in their own identity; the assistant neither performs nor observes it.
   Do not ask for numbers, expiry, CVC, authentication codes or screenshots in chat.
   The public source does not name this Company form's final button: do not
   invent **Save**, **Add**, or an automatic-save contract. If its destination or
   requested action is unclear, the operator stops before submission and asks
   Support; do not redirect the card to a personal profile or guess an owner.
4. Once private entry is over and inspection can safely resume, refresh/reopen
   the **same Company**. Have the operator privately compare the intended masked
   card and confirm it is active rather than pending. Do not expose full card
   details or rely only on a toast or a pre-existing **Saved** label.

## Completion and pending state

Report saved-state read back only for the intended card on that same Company.
Without authorized inspection, say **operator-reported**. Missing, ambiguous or
pending state is incomplete: read back before retrying so another card is not
added accidentally. A pending card is not ready for future charges.

The pending-card article requires the cardholder to finish bank authentication
in their own identity; this is separate from the operator-owned Company session.
It does not establish how a personal Customer Portal addition is routed to a
Company; obtain supported Company-specific help if that destination is unclear.
Do not impersonate the cardholder or collect their bank codes.

Adding a card does not charge it, pay a reservation, grant payment authority or
transfer its ownership. Never open/submit a charge, send a payment link, or create
a booking merely to test setup. Contact-owned and Company-linked card choices
can coexist for a Company reservation; charge authorization is a separate task.
