# Trip Rules, promo codes and service extras

Reviewed 2026-10-16 (Trip Rules calendar and Anytime zone choices); other sections 2026-10-15. Rate activation and booking changes belong to the operator.

## Trip Rules

The public guide calls Trip Rules the feature formerly named **Dynamic Pricing**.
The former name routes older-menu questions here; it does not establish that
separate marketing claims of AI real-time-demand adjustment describe this
workflow. See **Advertised AI demand-responsive pricing** below.

**Settings > Trip Rules > Create Trip Rule**. Public prerequisites include vehicle
BRA and Customer Portal display enabled. Ask which approved business condition
is intended: Anytime, Specific Date, Date Range or Time of Day.

Review the intended mode, dates/time window, affected vehicles, increase/decrease
and dollar/percent amount. **Date Range** also selects the weekdays within the
range; **Time of Day** selects applicable weekdays for the time window. For
**Specific Date** and **Date Range**, review the separate repeat-or-not choice
after vehicle selection. Do not assume a repeat cadence or offer repeat for
every mode. Compare mode, dates/times, weekdays, repetition and vehicles before
operator activation and again in the saved rule. Anytime can **Skip pricing** to
add notes or restrictions instead. In Help's **Anytime** flow, choose **All locations**
or **Only within custom zone**. For a custom zone, explicitly choose whether
**pickup**, **dropoff**, **both pickup and dropoff**, or **pickup or dropoff** must
match; both and either are not equivalent. Review optional Driver note, Trip note
and Restrict vehicles. If a custom zone was chosen, search its location on the
map, click points for its polygon and close the shape. Repeat for additional zones
only if intended, then continue to the overview. The public text does not prove
these location options apply identically to every date mode. Map drawing and
activation are operator edits, not navigation. Review the rule's name, timing,
vehicles, price/skip choice, matching and zones before operator **Activate**, then
inspect the saved rule and an eligible calculation.

Rules crossing midnight require two time windows; rules crossing calendar years
also require separate ranges per the public guide. Check gaps/overlap at the
boundary rather than assuming one wrapped interval works. Do not silently create
duplicate rules or quote a stacking order not established by the documentation.
On a reservation use **Price > Fare Breakdown** or the visible equivalent to
review applied adjustments. Never charge to test a rule.

## Promo codes

Confirm the intended company, promo access, approved discount and date/usage
policy. Creating a code makes an offer available; it is not a harmless preview.
The public November 2025 demonstration supplies creation details absent from the
Help Center body; current controls and validation may differ.

1. **Settings > Customer Portal > Promo Codes > Create Promo Code**.
2. Choose **Anytime**, **Booking date** (when the customer books), or **Trip date**
   (when the ride occurs). A trip booked today for next month is not today's trip
   date. Choose a fixed dollar discount or percentage and enter the code.
   The demonstrated percentage discount uses the **base rate**, not all charges.
3. For date-based codes, set the intended start/end dates. Review the actual date
   basis, range and value before proceeding; do not assume an expiration time,
   timezone, stacking rule or unsupported audience restriction.
4. Set **Total limit** and **Booking contact limit**, including Unlimited only
   when intended. Do not accept demonstrated example limits as business policy.
   Follow current form validation; do not guess allowed characters or bypass an
   error. Operator reviews and submits **Create Promo Code**.
5. Reopen the code from the list and compare saved date basis/range, value and
   limits with the approved offer. Select an existing code there to edit settings.
   Codes cannot be renamed. Both limits count lifetime use; editing limits does
   not reset usage. Do not create a replacement merely to evade those counts.

Codes can also be added like other pricing items to operator reservations/quotes;
customers enter them when reserving or confirming an operator quote. In the
demonstrated Customer Portal, enter the code and **Apply**, then inspect the
displayed discount against the base rate for a percentage offer. This verifies a
calculation, not a completed booking or redemption. Do not submit a reservation,
send a campaign or charge as an unapproved test.

For already-authorized bookings, reopen the code's **Results** to review
redemptions, total amount and promo amount. The webinar shows redemption after
reservation submission, not merely after Apply. Keep customer details out of chat.
If the form, calculation or saved offer differs, stop and resolve it before
distributing the code; do not promise eligibility or success from a toast alone.

## Meet & Greet

**Settings > General > Preferences > Pricing Layout > Add Pricing**, configure
Meet & Greet and its price, operator turns it on/submits. This affects customer
selection and trip pricing/communications, not only a cosmetic label.

Airport Pick-Up must be selected for Meet & Greet applicability. Review whether
curbside or inside service is intended and agreed amount. Operator-created
reservation can adjust populated price. Confirm service selection, price and
driver/passenger-visible instructions after submission. Do not enable service or
change pricing on an existing booking merely to demonstrate the option.

[Meet & Greet](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/7035469).

## Always-inside airport pickup: mandatory adjustment, not optional service

February's alternative is for an approved policy that every matching ride uses
inside pickup. A Trip Rule raises the base rate for the selected vehicles,
applicable dates/weekdays and airport pickup zone; matching rides receive the
adjustment automatically. It is not a selectable per-booking Meet & Greet service
or a separate Meet & Greet fee line. The later Q&A narrows the scope to selected
individual vehicles, not categories, and explicitly excludes optionality.

First decide whether inside service is mandatory or optional. For a mandatory
policy, use the Trip Rules steps above to review approved dates, vehicles, amount
and pickup-zone match; operator reviews/activates and reads back the rule and
permitted matching/nonmatching fare calculations. Do not copy the demo amount.
The demo turns off the ordinary Meet & Greet choice to remove the curbside/inside
selection. Do not do that globally without reviewing every affected booking
choice; a targeted fare rule does not establish targeted service-toggle behavior.
If service must remain optional, this mandatory adjustment is not the solution.

## Advertised AI demand-responsive pricing

Reviewed 2026-10-16. The [December 4, 2024 AI post](https://www.moovsapp.com/post/using-ai-for-chauffeured-transportation-check-out-moovs)
explicitly advertises rates automatically adjusting to real-time demand, time
and location. That is a broader advertised function than the documented
operator-configured Trip Rules above; the former Dynamic Pricing name does not
prove a demand-sensing optimizer is the same feature or currently available.

**Documentation gap:** this source does not show current activation or how to
configure/apply the advertised demand-based adjustments. Ask Moovs for supported
instructions and the intended account scope. Do not erase the advertised claim,
invent an AI pricing switch, approximate demand by editing live rules or promise
revenue/price outcomes. Trip Rules coaching remains the documented rule-based
workflow; no booking or charge is authorized to test either function.
