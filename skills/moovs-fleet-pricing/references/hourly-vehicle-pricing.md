# Configure a vehicle's non-tiered Hourly schedule

Reviewed 2026-10-16 from September2025 literal product-training footage and
narration, not current production UI or proof of account entitlement. Operator
performs every field, mode/toggle, day selection and Save. This configures vehicle
rates; it does not create an Hourly trip, apply a quote rate or collect money.

1. Confirm company, exact vehicle, approved weekday/weekend rates, minimum hours,
   weekend days and deadhead policy. Open **Vehicles > vehicle > Pricing** and
   scroll to **Hourly**. Check actual access and editable state first.
   The [deadhead Help](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6387682-deadhead-deadhead-rate-per-mile-and-total-deadhead-duration-overview-what-is-deadhead)
   says BRA off before editing; reconcile differing current controls before
   changing anything. Do not toggle automation merely to inspect this form.
2. Review **Covered Deadhead Duration**. The recorded selector offers hour values,
   **0 Hours** and **Deadhead disabled** as distinct choices. Operator selects
   only the approved policy. Zero free hours is not disabled deadhead. The older
   narration assumes a company garage; use the actual assigned garage and do not
   override [documented formula/garage conflicts](base-pricing.md#explain-the-rate-not-just-the-final-number).
3. For a non-tiered **WEEKDAYS** schedule, operator sets **Hourly Minimum** and
   **Hourly Rate**, checking **Per hour** rather than accidentally using **Flat**.
   Confirm the intended units, not the demo's example prices. If **Tiered Pricing**
   is on, stop to review the existing bands and intended conversion; do not turn
   it off as incidental cleanup or assume rates survive a mode change. For an
   intentional weekday tier policy, use the [tiered editor](tiered-vehicle-pricing.md).
4. Under **WEEKENDS**, operator uses **Choose Your Weekends** to select the agreed
   weekdays, then sets that schedule's **Hourly Minimum**, **Hourly Rate** and
   **Per hour** mode. Friday-Sunday is a demo example, not a required definition.
   A weekend can differ in minimum hours, rate or both. Apply the same tier-mode
   caution here; weekday and weekend settings are separate.
5. Review all intended values and any other pending vehicle edits before the
   operator selects the visible **Save**. Do not persist unrelated Transfer,
   policy or portal changes left in the drawer. Reopen the same vehicle's Pricing
   and compare both schedules, day selection and covered-deadhead choice.
   A Saved indicator is not a substitute for this readback; uncertain save needs
   inspection before retry. Without permitted readback, label operator-reported.

Customer Portal display/BRA enablement are separate settings and permissions;
this save alone does not establish public prices or direct booking. A separately
authorized synthetic preview can compare an intended weekday and weekend fare
without submitting. Do not promise retrospective repricing, a universal hourly
minimum formula or public display from configuration alone.
