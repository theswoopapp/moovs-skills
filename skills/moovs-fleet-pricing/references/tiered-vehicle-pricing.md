# Configure vehicle Transfer Fixed tiers or weekday Hourly tiers

Reviewed 2026-10-16 from September2025 literal product footage plus current
[pricing Help](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/9718727-understanding-pricing-in-the-moovs-app).
This is dated vehicle configuration, not current UI or account-access verification.
Use the [BRA configuration checks](base-pricing.md#configure-bra-deliberately),
including actual editability and portal impact. Operator owns every edit and Save.
Ask for the intended vehicle, branch and complete approved band policy; do not
change modes just to explore them or copy the training example's prices.

## Transfer Fixed-tier bands

1. Open the intended **Vehicles > vehicle > Pricing**, review the existing
   Transfer/deadhead settings and other pending vehicle edits. For the agreed
   policy, operator selects Transfer **Tiered Pricing > Fixed tier**.
2. The recorded Fixed editor shows intervals such as **0 to … miles**, followed
   by **… to … miles** and an open-ended **…+ miles** row. Operator enters the
   approved interval limits, amounts and each row's **Per mile** or **Flat** unit;
   **+ Add Tier** adds a band. These are interval limits, not the **Next** band's
   incremental length. Review continuity and the actual current form's validation;
   the recording does not settle which band owns an exact shared boundary.
3. **Fixed tier is not the same as Flat.** Current Help selects a band using the
   whole trip distance, then applies that band's rate to the whole distance.
   It explicitly gives a per-mile Fixed example. A Flat row instead expresses
   a fixed band amount. The recording narrates fixed-dollar examples while
   **Per mile** remains selected on screen: do not copy those examples or promise
   their totals. Confirm both band mode and row unit against the approved policy.
4. Review the entire intended vehicle change, then operator chooses **Save** once.
   Reopen the same vehicle Pricing and compare Fixed mode, all intervals/amounts/
   units and unchanged deadhead/Hourly/portal settings. A training Save click is
   not proof the operator's values persisted. Inspect before retrying an uncertain save.

## Weekday Hourly Incremental bands

1. On that vehicle's **Pricing**, scroll to **Hourly > WEEKDAYS**. Review
   **Covered Deadhead Duration**, existing minimum and the intended weekday policy.
   For the approved change, operator enables weekday **Tiered Pricing** and selects
   **Incremental** under **Hourly Rate**. Do not change the weekend schedule incidentally.
2. Review the separate **Deadhead Hourly Rate** and its unit. Covered duration is
   a free allowance, not the trip band length; **0 Hours** is not **Deadhead disabled**.
   Current Help adds separately calculated deadhead to the tiered base component;
   retain the [published calculation conflicts](base-pricing.md#explain-the-rate-not-just-the-final-number).
3. Operator fills **First … hours**, each **Next … hours**, and **Remaining hours**,
   setting each approved amount and **Per hour** or **Flat** unit. **+ Add Tier**
   adds a **Next** band. Here Next means that band's additional duration, not a
   cumulative endpoint. Incremental pricing uses each band's portion; do not use
   the last rate across all reserved hours or infer minimum interactions not documented.
4. Review all bands, units, deadhead rate/allowance, minimum and unchanged weekend/
   Transfer settings, then operator selects **Save** once. Reopen the same vehicle
   and compare saved values. Weekend day selection and non-tiered minimum/rate
   setup have a separate [walkthrough](hourly-vehicle-pricing.md); this recording
   does not demonstrate the weekend-tier or Hourly Fixed editor as a complete branch.

After either save, an independently authorized synthetic unsent trip can inspect
**BRA Calculations** where offered and compare the agreed inputs/band components,
separate deadhead and total. Do not click to apply a suggested base rate, send a
booking or enable portal automation merely to verify a setting. Exact thresholds,
minimum/zone/Trip Rule interactions and historical bookings need their own checks;
configuration alone does not prove public prices or retrospective repricing.
