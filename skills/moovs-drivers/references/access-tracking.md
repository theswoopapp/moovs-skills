# Driver profiles, access and tracking

Reviewed 2026-10-16 (access/device, app overview and customer trip-page details);
other sections 2026-10-15. Operator controls all profile changes, links and messages.

## Add or check the driver

Open **Settings > Drivers**, then the appropriate profile or creation control.
Published required profile fields are first name and cell phone number. Ask the
operator to enter/update the number privately, including country code; avoid
creating duplicates while diagnosing an existing profile. Read back the intended
profile after save. Selecting a driver in reservation Dispatch is an assignment
that can send an accept/decline request, not a profile-preview action.

[Adding a driver](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6472581)

## Connect the Driver App

Before sign-in/use, confirm the intended profile/mobile and Driver App activation
in **Moovs Market**. Installation alone neither proves access nor needs a purchase
or activation performed by the assistant.
The driver installs **Moovs Driver**, published by Swoop, from the official store.
They sign in using the mobile number on their Moovs profile and SMS six-digit
code. They enter the code privately, never through the assistant. Allow required
location and notification permissions; camera is relevant for shuttle QR scans.

If more than one company is linked: driver uses **More > Switch Accounts** and
chooses the intended company. Only already-linked companies appear; an absent
company needs its linkage checked/Support, not an invented in-app account-link flow.
Do not assume another company's trips are missing.
For sign-in failure: check profile/country code, SMS reception, latest app and
automatic phone date/time. Close/reopen as appropriate before reassessing.
Request a resend once, use newest code, then wait if attempts were repeated.
For “No Access to Driver App,” the operator checks Market activation; after an
authorized activation is resolved separately, driver reopens/refreshes the app.
Escalate via **?** in Operator with device/error and masked number, not a code.

## Place driver-visible notes

Use location-specific **Notes** for stop instructions; **Driver Notes** for general
reservation/passenger instructions. Both are driver-visible. Operator enters
only necessary operational details, never private administrative commentary.
After operator save, reopen the intended reservation/stop and compare the note
and its placement. Saved placement is not proof the driver read it.

## Copy and privately share a Passenger link

Reservation > selected trip > **Links > Passenger**; smaller screens may show
**More > View Links**. Farmed-in trips do not show Passenger link. Operator
copies/shares privately with the intended passenger after confirming the exact
trip and recipient; never paste the link into chat/public notes. Copy is not
proof of delivery. The passenger opens it and uses **Driver Tracking > View Map**;
confirming the intended trip page is not proof live tracking has started.
The customer can review current trip/driver status and passenger, trip, stop and
vehicle details. **Driver Contact** offers call/text; the customer decides and
privately initiates any contact in their own identity. Opening the card is not a
call, sent text or driver response. Do not request their link, log in as them or
copy driver phone/location into chat. Never contact a driver as a tracking test.

## Diagnose missing live driver tracking

Check the correct assigned driver and trip link. Live location begins after the
driver starts the trip in the Driver App and continues while it is active, with
location access enabled and a working data connection. Ask the driver to confirm
these prerequisites safely, not while driving. A pre-trip page without current
location is not by itself a failure; never start a trip merely to force tracking.
After a permitted correction, review the intended map's updating location when
available. If it still does not update, use **?** in Operator to contact Support;
without direct readback, label the result operator-reported.

[Driver App overview](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6411777), [Passenger tracking](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8336663)

## Device troubleshooting boundaries

For a white screen/crash, first consider update/reopen and establish the device
platform. Driver must be safely stationary and control their own device/sign-in.
Explain the relevant local-state/sign-in effect and confirm recovery access
before clearing/deleting anything; do not perform device changes for them.

- **Android** public path: **Settings > Apps/App Management > Moovs Driver >
  Storage Usage**, then the offered **Storage & cache > Clear Cache > Clear Data**.
  Menu names vary. Driver reviews data-clearing consequences before acting,
  reopens the app and signs in privately if prompted; no codes in chat.
- **iOS** public path: **Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Moovs Driver >
  Offload App**. Apple distinguishes offloading (keeps documents/data) from
  deletion (removes app/related data). Driver can tap the offloaded Home Screen
  app icon to re-download, then reopen. If Offload is unavailable, Moovs gives
  **Delete App** followed by official App Store reinstall as an alternative;
  explain its different data effect before a deliberate choice, not an automatic
  next step. Private sign-in may still be needed; no guaranteed session recovery.

After the driver acts, review whether the app opens and the reported symptom
persists. Do not start/change a real trip to test. Recovery attempt is not a
verified fix; pause on materially different controls and seek current support.

For requested iPhone alert preferences, use only matching offered controls from
the older public guide; do not require unrelated device changes for a trip review:
- Louder alerts: **Settings > Sounds & Haptics**, then the notification volume slider.
- Visible alerts: **Settings > Notifications > Moovs Driver**; driver chooses
  intended **Lock Screen / Notification Center / Banners** and **Persistent**
  banner style when offered. Preference saved is not proof an alert was delivered.
- Only if spoken alerts are requested: **Settings > Notifications > Announce
  Notifications**, enable that feature and the offered Moovs Driver announcement
  setting. Do not enable this optional preference on the driver's behalf.

The older guide's push-to-text fallback/delay is not a guaranteed current service
contract. Do not disable push as a generic fix, especially when Auto Dispatch
needs it. Its truncated final tip supplies no additional supported step.
