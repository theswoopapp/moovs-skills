# Relay a driver-owned one-way trip walkthrough

Reviewed 2026-10-16 against the complete undated public Driver App text demo,
steps20–35. This is dated training guidance, not an inspected current app screen
or a guarantee of controls, notifications, GPS or saved state. Read matching
current Help when available; stop on materially different controls/state rather
than recreating a plausible screen.

## One-way progress and driver closeout

An operator can relay these instructions; the intended driver controls their own
signed-in app and every trip event/entry/Submit. Neither the assistant nor the
operator impersonates the driver. Coordinate while safely stationary, not while
driving. Confirm the intended company, assigned trip/date/route and actual Driver
App access without requesting the driver's codes, phone, private link or location.
Do not activate, start or progress a real ride just to test tracking.

1. Driver opens the intended trip using **Go to Trip** and privately checks its
   details. Do not choose another company's or another leg's trip to match a demo.
2. Only when actually leaving toward pickup, driver chooses **Start Trip**. The
   demo describes **On the Way** and location on afterward; read the real state,
   not a promise that location, ETA or customer notifications are working.
3. At the actual pickup arrival, driver uses **I arrived at pickup location** if
   offered for that state. When the passenger is in the vehicle and departure is
   appropriate, driver chooses **Go to Drop-Off Location**. Do not advance events
   in advance or record passenger boarding that has not happened.
4. At actual destination arrival, driver uses **I arrived at Drop-off Location**.
   The demo makes **End Trip** timing company-dependent: passenger exit or return
   to the company's location. Confirm the company's approved meaning; do not
   impose garage return, premature completion or a universal end-time rule.
5. For the subsequent driver closeout, the demo asks for actual pickup and
   passenger drop-off times, then **Submit**. Driver privately checks accurate
   service times and submits only the intended trip. Do not substitute estimated
   times or copy example values. If the current closeout requests different fields
   or date/time meaning is unclear, reconcile before submission.
6. Driver checks the intended trip's resulting progress/closeout information when
   available and reports discrepancies without sharing private trip data. This is
   a recommended result check, not a demo-proven persistence screen or delivery.
   If outcome is uncertain, inspect existing state before another event/Submit.

The text demo has erroneous repeated pickup instructions at steps25/27/29/31,
including after drop-off/end narration. Do not follow those as extra pickup
arrivals or pretend they establish a coherent extra transition. Use real state
and the unambiguous surrounding stages above; pause for supported instructions
if they conflict. Step23 mentions **Undo**, but does not establish its effects
or reversal of notifications; never use it as a diagnostic or guaranteed rollback.

For incidental stops/charges, July's Q&A describes the driver reporting details
in closeout notes, not adding a charge directly. If that field is offered, the
driver records actual context; do not invent a note-entry button from captions.
Operator review then uses the [completed-trip closeout](payroll.md#review-and-close-out-a-completed-trip-before-financial-handoffs)
and separately approved [incidentals/overages](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6609810-charging-a-customer-for-incidentals-overages-after-they-ve-paid)
workflow. A driver note is neither customer payment consent nor a price change
or collected charge; compare the reviewed price and actual payment separately.
[July 2026, 42:46-43:49](https://fast.wistia.com/embed/iframe/64e8h1tobn?wtime=2566s).

Driver-submitted closeout is not the Operator **Close Trip** financial workflow,
customer payment, driver payment or approval of earnings. Keep those separate;
do not close again, charge, pay or alter status to force a label. Without direct
permitted result inspection, completion remains driver/operator-reported.

## Choose a map for an ordinary Driver Link trip

Reviewed 2026-10-16. August2025 literal browser screen-sharing shows the Driver
Link's Trip Planner and navigation menu, not a current native-phone inspection.
The external map opening and copied address bytes are not shown. Current Driver
App Help supports navigating to stops; use the dated controls below only when
those actually offered match. This is not Shuttle traffic/navigation setup.

1. The safely stationary driver opens their own already-assigned trip's Driver
   Link and checks company, trip/date and intended pickup or drop-off. If a link
   is needed, the operator uses the existing reservation's selected trip >
   **Links > Driver** (mobile: **More > View Links > Driver**) and shares it only
   with that driver after review. Do not assign a driver, use another identity,
   start a trip or send a test link merely to expose navigation.
2. In the Driver Link, driver scrolls to **Trip Planner** and compares the intended
   stop's address with the actual service instructions. Each displayed pickup/
   drop-off row has its own blue circular direction-arrow button. Driver opens
   the arrow for the correct stop, not the passenger call/text buttons or the
   bottom trip-status bar. The recording opens the pickup row's menu.
3. The demonstrated menu lists **Google Maps**, **Apple Maps**, **Waze** and
   **Copy Address**. Driver chooses the intended offered map themselves and
   reviews any device/browser handoff prompt before continuing. Use the actual
   resulting map interface; do not invent an app-specific confirmation button,
   promise an installed app will launch, or claim a default preference was saved.
4. In the resulting map, driver privately checks that its destination matches
   the intended stop, including the correct location/entrance when relevant,
   before starting directions or moving. An opened chooser is not a completed
   handoff. If the map does not open or the destination differs, stop and resolve
   it; do not advance a trip status or edit a real booking to force navigation.
5. For another map or an unavailable direct handoff, driver can choose the offered
   **Copy Address**, open their chosen map and paste it into its destination
   search privately. Check the actual pasted/matched destination before using
   directions; a closed menu or copy toast alone does not verify clipboard text.
   If copying/pasting fails, obtain supported help rather than repeatedly changing
   trip state, installing software or granting new device permissions as a test.

Finish by stating whether the correct destination was actually inspected in the
chosen map, copied but not verified, or blocked. Return to the same Driver Link
when needed for real service progress; map selection is not evidence of Start
Trip, location tracking, arrival, driver acceptance or a completed ride. Without
permitted direct readback, report the driver's result as driver-reported.
