# Communicate approved proposed pay in Driver Notes

Reviewed 2026-10-16. This enriches driver-note placement, not a new payroll task.
January2025 narration suggests writing the proposed payout in **Driver Notes** so
the intended driver can review it before accepting/declining. It narrates that
text in the Driver Link/App; captions are not independently inspected UI frames
or proof of current account behavior. Current public Help confirms general Driver
Notes are driver-visible and distinct from location-specific stop Notes.

1. Confirm company, exact reservation/trip, intended driver and business-approved
   proposed amount/currency and wording. Do not invent pay terms, copy demo numbers
   or change pay defaults. A conceptual explanation needs no payroll-record audit.
2. Review the current Driver Notes field and audience. Operator places only the
   approved necessary text there, using the offered edit/save controls. Do not
   place it in customer-facing Trip Notes, stop Notes or private Internal Comments
   as a substitute. Stop if the field's audience or persistence is unclear.
3. Reopen the intended trip's Driver Notes and compare the exact approved text
   and placement. For a separately requested recipient-side check, the intended
   driver reviews their own link/app in their own identity; never obtain their
   private link or impersonate them. Operator readback alone does not prove
   driver receipt, reading or acceptance; manual results are operator-reported.

A note is communication, not calculated earnings, a changed payout/default,
recorded payable, remittance or proof of a pay agreement. Do not assign/farm out,
accept an assignment, close a trip or process payables just to show the note.
For ordinary note placement, use [driver-note guidance](access-tracking.md#place-driver-visible-notes);
for a separately requested financial change, use [payroll](payroll.md).
