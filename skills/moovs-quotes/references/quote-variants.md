# Quote-trip copies and Hourly quote creation

Reviewed 2026-10-16. These public 2025/2026 product captures and instructions
are dated coaching, not current account validation. Confirm company, exact quote/trip and
purpose first. Apply [task authority](task-authority.md) to entries, selections and submission;
editable fields may save immediately. Guided mode gives one step, then waits. No browser:
manual guidance and operator-reported results, not independently verified success.

## Duplicate one trip inside an existing quote

Use this for an additional intended service trip, not mutually exclusive vehicle
alternatives or a new quote copied from the whole request.

1. On **Quotes**, locate the intended quote and open its exact trip row. Compare
   reference, contact, itinerary/date and price before copying it.
2. In the trip drawer, operator opens the upper-right three-dot menu and chooses
   **Duplicate Trip**. Confirm the dialog is for that trip, not the whole quote.
3. Operator selects only the agreed date(s) in **Select all dates to duplicate
   on**. The dated dialog starts with a date that may differ from the source trip;
   do not accept it without checking. Review intended number of copies/date(s),
   then the authorized actor selects **Duplicate** once.
4. Return to the same quote and open the new trip reference(s). Compare each
   copied trip's date/time, stops, passenger/contact, vehicle, price and any
   dispatch details with the intended service, preserving the original. The demo
   returns to one quote containing two distinct trip references. A toast alone
   is not complete readback; if uncertain, inspect existing trips before retry.
5. Copying is not sending, customer acceptance or reservation conversion. Review
   the quote's combined services/total before any separately requested send.

## Prepare an Hourly quote's timing and inspect its calculation

This is a positive **preparation/calculation** path, not proof of complete Hourly
quote creation, all required fields or a saved/sent quote. The footage deliberately
leaves contact/order information blank to inspect pricing; do not treat those
blanks as valid final submission or skip needed booking details for a real quote.

1. Confirm the intended Hourly service, pickup/end dates/times, mapped route,
   vehicle and whether the task is preparation only. On **Quotes > Create**,
   confirm **Create New Quote** and have the operator select **Hourly** Trip Type.
   Order Type is separate; do not substitute a service reason for Hourly.
2. Operator enters **Pick-up Date & Time** and **Drop-Off Date & Time**. Review
   both dates, AM/PM, applicable time zone, chronology and intended duration.
   An hourly price or billed-hours value is not the scheduled end time.
3. Operator selects mapped pickup/drop-off addresses and intended stops, uses
   **Add Vehicle**, chooses the agreed vehicle, then confirms **Add Vehicle**
   in that dialog. Selection does not establish availability or assign a driver.
4. Review pricing's **BRA Calculations** where offered. The dated popup shows an
   **Hourly Rate** breakdown and total while the Base Rate field remains blank.
   Explain hours/bands and approved inputs; opening the breakdown is not applying
   that suggestion. Do not click the rate-application icon or edit vehicle rates
   merely to inspect a quote. Close the popup and compare all intended quote data.
5. Stop before final submission for preparation-only scope: **prepared, not saved**.
   The inspected sequence does not save this Hourly quote. Do not infer its final
   Save/Send behavior from the One Way example; use supported current controls
   and validation before any separately intended operator save, then reopen the
   saved reference and verify Hourly, both endpoint times, route, vehicle and price.

## Create and save an Hourly quote

Reviewed 2026-10-16. Public quote-creation/save instructions and the literal
Hourly **Create New Quote** form support this complete dated coaching path.
February demonstrates timing and calculation without submitting; July shows the
shared quote-save controls; August explicitly explains saving a new quote for
review before sending. This is combined public guidance, not footage of a
completed Hourly submission or current tenant/browser acceptance.

For calculation-only preparation, use the section above and stop before save;
that task does not require a completed booking contact or authorize saving/sending.
The steps below are for an intended complete quote with those business details.

1. Confirm company, new **Hourly quote** (not a reservation), intended existing
   booking contact/passenger, service schedule/route, approved vehicle/price,
   and preparation only versus saving without an email. Search for an existing
   matching quote first. On **Quotes > Create**, confirm **Create New Quote**.
   Coach in guided mode; in execution perform the approved entries/selections/save.
2. In **Search for booking contact**, operator types the intended existing name,
   waits for results, compares name/email/phone, then selects the right result.
   Compare **Booking / Billing Contact**, any linked **Company**, and intended
   **Order Type**. Review **Passenger** separately. Public
   [Linked Passengers guidance](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/9682073-linked-passengers)
   supports replacing the passenger with the contact's linked passenger or another
   existing contact. Operator uses the offered controls and compares the resulting
   passenger card; selecting billing contact alone does not prove the right rider.
3. Operator selects **Hourly** Trip Type, separate from Order Type, then enters
   **Pick-up Date & Time** and **Drop-Off Date & Time**. Compare both dates,
   AM/PM, applicable local time zones, chronology and intended service duration.
   A billed-hours value is not the scheduled end time. Hourly end time does not
   add a separately scheduled return leg.
4. Operator selects mapped pickup and drop-off addresses, adding only intended
   intermediate stops with **Add Stop**. Compare the resulting addresses and stop
   order. Review **Passenger Count**, **Trip Notes** and **Luggage** for the
   actual service; keep internal information out of customer-facing notes.
5. Operator uses **Add Vehicle**, selects the approved vehicle, then confirms
   **Add Vehicle** in the dialog. Compare the selected vehicle and capacity.
   This is not availability acceptance or driver assignment.
6. Review **Base Rate**, offered **Add Pricing** / **Add Promo Code**, tax,
   fee/gratuity lines and **Total**. Operator enters approved quote pricing and
   checks the resulting total. If inspecting **BRA Calculations**, explain the
   offered Hourly breakdown; opening it does not apply its suggested price.
   Do not leave an intended final rate blank merely because the demo did, copy
   demo rates, or alter fleet/global pricing to finish this individual quote.
7. Review the entire quote and **Internal Comments**. For preparation only, stop
   before submission: **prepared, not saved**. To save without this quote email,
   operator opens the offered split menu, deliberately selects **Save quote**
   rather than **Save quote & send email**, verifies the main button's label,
   and submits once. Address current validation on this same form before retrying;
   never substitute a reservation or send/convert/enroll to make saving work.
   A requested email is a separate deliberate recipient/action review, not an
   automatic follow-up. Save-only does not promise suppression of all notifications.
8. Use **View Quote** if offered or locate the saved quote, then reopen the exact
   reference. Compare contact/passenger, Hourly, both endpoint dates/times,
   route/stops, vehicle, price/total and visible quote status; open **View Trip**
   for details missing from the summary. A toast alone is not completion. If
   saving times out, find the existing record before another create/save.
   Report **saved-state read back**, **operator-reported** without authorized
   browser readback, or **unknown/blocked** when persistence cannot be established.
   Saving is not email delivery, customer agreement, payment or conversion.
