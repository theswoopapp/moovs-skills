## Create a Shuttle company

Confirm intended company and existing records before **Contacts > Companies >
Add New Company**. Operator enters required **Company Name**. Logo, phone, email,
address, website and description are optional available details, not mandatory
fields for every company. Operator saves. Reopen the company and compare its name and
intended details; a saved company does not create/publish a route. Separate
companies when passengers require distinct links/branding, not to troubleshoot
a missing route or duplicate an existing company.

[Company setup](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8430071)

## Customize route branding

Open **Shuttle Routes > intended route > Branding**. Confirm company/route,
approved logo/colors and permission before the operator edits. Logo formats are
PNG/JPG/SVG, maximum 2 MB; recommended 200 x 40 pixels. **Primary Color** controls
headers/primary buttons; **Accent Color** controls links/secondary buttons/outlines.
Operator reviews and saves. **Reset to Defaults** intentionally restores original
colors; it is not a general undo. Stop on material UI/save-behavior differences.
Reopen Branding and compare saved logo and both colors; privately review the
correct passenger experience when authorized, without creating a booking or
sending a link/email. Inspect current state before retrying an uncertain save.
Without direct inspection, readback is operator-reported. Saved logo/colors do
not prove every cached page changed. Shared display/support details remain a
[separate source gap](shared-shuttle.md#shared-route-branding), not a condition
for this bounded task.

## Build and publish the right route

**Shuttle Routes > Create shuttle route**. Confirm company and route type.
For Fixed Schedule, review Route Details, Stops, Route Version & Vehicles and
Review & Create; Stop-to-Stop adds Stop Groups & Pricing and optional return
route. Choose Stop Time Select versus Stop-to-Stop explicitly. Self Check-In is
an optional passenger action for supported bookings, not a driver assignment.

Continuous Loop needs ordered stops, service dates/days/hours, frequency,
vehicles and applicable driver breaks. Shared Shuttle needs common location,
eligible area, pricing zones, operating days, buffers and permitted location types.
Operator reviews and chooses actual **Create Route/Publish** action; Shared
Shuttle publishes from **Review & Publish**. Never publish sample operations.

Finish applicable Ticket Management (passenger types, fares/packages/items,
gratuity), Branding, passenger and booking setup. Shared Shuttle can also expose
Clients/Imports and Scheduler Board; consult the actual public guide/form for
specific import format rather than uploading a customer file to explore it.

Changing a published route can create/update a version before becoming active.
Confirm effective date/version and review future operations/links after changes.
Do not claim all existing bookings updated because a route edit saved.

For the ordinary initial Loop/Fixed input, review, authorized Create Route and saved
route chain, use [builder timing and creation](builder-timing.md), expanded
2026-10-16 against literal December2025 public frames and current Help. It is not
current production acceptance; the original caption-only review remains history.

The whole multi-route task remains incomplete: detailed Shared configuration and
Stop-to-Stop pricing/return choices are not completed by the Loop/Fixed capture.
Obtain supported guidance at a material undocumented choice rather than guess.
Version activation, overlaps and existing-booking effects remain separate for
published-route edits, not prerequisites for ordinary initial creation.

## Check route Edit/Delete role limits

The June 2026 demonstration says route **Edit** and **Delete** are available to
Owner/Admin and hidden from Members. This is a route-action boundary, not proof
Members cannot view Shuttle or perform every operational task. Page-access
settings and these action restrictions are different checks.

Confirm the current user's role, intended route and visible action before
coaching an edit. Do not elevate permissions or switch identities to reveal it;
ask the authorized Owner/Admin when the route action is unavailable. Execute
only the specifically authorized action in the permitted role. Deletion effects, recovery
and confirmation controls are not established here; stop rather than invent a
delete walkthrough. For supported edits, recommend reopening the intended route,
effective version and future Operations; a saved route is not proof old bookings
changed. This readback is a safety recommendation, not a demonstrated delete test.

## Review paired return-route scope

The route guide allows an optional return pairing for Fixed Schedule
Stop-to-Stop Search. September 2026 also describes fixed return-route pairing
for round-trip bookings. This is not a second Shared Shuttle trip or a
Continuous Loop seat reservation.

Identify the intended company, outbound/return routes, stops, service dates and
current versions before discussing pairing. The public sources do not establish
the exact pair/unpair controls, compatibility checks or effects on existing
bookings. Obtain current supported guidance rather than guessing a toggle or
editing live routes. Operator performs any later approved pairing/save/publish.
Recommended readback is both saved route associations/effective dates and the
intended return choices in the authorized experience, without submitting a test
booking. An offered round-trip choice alone does not prove correct paired
inventory, payment or existing-booking migration. Report unread states unknown.

## Review daily Shuttle Operations

Open the intended route > **Operations**, then choose service date. Inspect
vehicles/drivers, passenger counts, progress and map; open the operation for
bookings or stop-by-stop timeline. Confirm exact operation, vehicle suitability
and driver before operator assigns/updates them through offered controls.
Reopen the same service date/operation and compare assignment and booking scope.
Assignment is not driver acceptance, passenger boarding or completed service;
do not change trip statuses to make a review look complete.

For **Continuous Loop**, December 2025 narration also describes opening the
completed operation to review capacity, then its log for total boarded/disembarked
and the stop event timeline's arrival/departure entries. Match route, service day, operation
and stop before comparing entries. These are recorded counts/events, not guaranteed
actual occupancy, complete telemetry or proof no one boarded when an entry is
missing. Review only; do not start/end a route, scan a pass or alter counts/statuses
to populate the log. Report missing/ambiguous data rather than fabricate events.
Exact log-opening controls are not established by captions; use current supported
controls, and label uninspected results operator-reported.

## Resolve missing operational dates

If the intended route has no operational dates, inspect its route version and
vehicle configuration. Confirm service dates, recurrence/operating hours and
vehicle setup before proposing an edit. Operator corrects only the demonstrated
configuration error using current controls. Reopen future **Operations** and
verify the intended service dates and vehicles; saved configuration alone is not
proof the right version is active or existing bookings changed. Do not publish
sample operations or shift actual service dates to force an empty screen to fill.

[Operational-date troubleshooting](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8430098)

## Copy a Shuttle passenger or booking link

From **Shuttle Routes**, operator chooses **Copy passenger app link** or **Copy
booking link**, then intended company/experience. Available choices depend on
configured routes. Passenger links: Fixed Schedule opens Stop Time Select;
Stop-to-Stop opens bookings; Loop opens the passenger route experience. Booking
links start Shared Shuttle or Stop-to-Stop booking; Loop opens the public map.

Privately verify destination company and experience before operator sends or
creates a QR code. Copying is not sending, delivery, a booking or payment. If a
company/route is missing, review existing company, active route, current version
and operational dates instead of creating duplicates. Never paste secure links
or passenger lists into chat/public artifacts.

[Link choices and visibility](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8430071),
[Booking experiences](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/9185249).
