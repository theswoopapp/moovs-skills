# Shuttle booking administration and handoffs

Reviewed 2026-10-15. Confirm purchased experience/access as explained in SKILL.md.
Operator submits all bookings, scheduling moves, passenger edits, fees, cards,
cancellations, refunds and messages. Pause inspection for payment/verification
entry. Never process a real test payment or act as the passenger/driver.

## Create a Shared Shuttle booking

Confirm active company/route, Ticket Management passenger types/prices, eligible
area/common location, operating days and buffers. Check for an existing booking.

1. Open **Shuttle Routes > Create booking**, choose company/Shared route, or open
   route > **Bookings > Create Booking**.
2. Operator selects/creates passenger and booking contact, enters eligible
   locations/date, counts, luggage, notes/ADA and requested airport flight details.
3. Review service time/fare before operator creates and completes any payment
   step privately. Reopen the booking and compare route, date, locations, passenger
   counts, price and actual payment state. Booking creation is not trip assignment.

[Operator booking guide](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8430112)

## Create a Stop-to-Stop booking

Confirm active Fixed Schedule Stop-to-Stop route, passenger types/fares, stops,
stop groups, operational dates and available trips. Stop Time Select bookings are
passenger-created, not this Operator flow.

1. Open route > **Bookings > Create Booking**.
2. Operator chooses One-way/Round Trip, origin/destination/date, available
   departure/return, passenger types/quantities/names, extras, contact and notes.
3. Review fare before operator submits booking/payment. Reopen the booking and
   compare chosen trips/stops/dates, passenger counts, total and payment history.
   A submitted booking is not proof payment settled or a passenger boarded.

[Stop-to-Stop booking](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8430112)

bounded standard booking guidance above.

## Find and review a Shuttle booking

Open the intended route > **Bookings**. Have the operator set applicable date,
status, route, zone, ADA, client or search filters; inspect active filters before
interpreting no results. Open the exact booking and compare passenger, service,
price and payment information with the requested record. Read only needed data;
do not export a passenger list or change a booking merely to locate it.

[Find bookings](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8430112)

## Edit or cancel a Shuttle booking

Locate the exact booking in route > **Bookings**. Review requested changes,
service date, passenger, price/payment state and affected trip before operator
uses the offered edit/cancel action. The guide does not establish every edit
field or confirmation label; stop if scope/validation is unclear. Reopen the same
booking and compare changed fields or cancellation status. Cancel does **not**
automatically refund; any refund is a separate reviewed financial action, not a
way to fix a label or retry uncertain cancellation.

[Edit/cancel booking](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8430112),
[Refund scope](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6620447)

review flow above, rather than resubmitting it. This dated limitation/request is

## Organize Shared Shuttle bookings into trips

Shared bookings can remain unassigned until placed on a trip. Open route >
**Scheduler Board** to inspect bookings and existing trips. Operator reviews
capacity, times/locations and affected bookings before grouping, moving or
ordering them. Use [the detailed Shared Shuttle guide](shared-shuttle.md) for the
public demonstration's scheduler flow; do not substitute Fixed Schedule steps.
After changes, inspect **Operations** and the intended trip's bookings/order and
actual assignments. Grouping alone does not prove a driver was notified/assigned.

[Scheduling scope](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8430112)

## Create or update a Shuttle passenger

Open route > **Passengers** and search the intended name, email or phone before
creating another record. Open that passenger, or operator chooses **Add Passenger**
when a new record is actually needed. Operator updates only requested contact
information using the current fields/save control. Reopen the intended profile
and compare those fields; a profile is not a booking or proof of passenger access.
Do not copy a person's contact information into chat or inspect unrelated profiles.

[Passenger records](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8430112)

## Manage Shuttle passenger cards

Route > **Passengers** > intended profile contains saved cards; maximum is 10.
Operator privately uses **Add Card**, the card menu for primary/delete, or **View
Payment Activity**. Review exact masked method and effect before each action;
primary changes the preference for future supported charges, not prior payments.
Pause inspection during card entry. Afterwards verify only the intended masked
card, primary state or relevant payment activity. Card addition is not collection;
never delete every method or charge merely to test readiness.

[Passenger cards](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8430112)

## Add a Stop-to-Stop fee

Open the exact Stop-to-Stop booking's payment section. **Add fee** charges the
payment method on file; it is not a harmless line-item preview. Operator reviews
fee name, positive amount, currency and intended card before entering/submitting.
Stop if the method or scope is unclear. Reopen the booking's payment information
and compare the fee, amount and resulting payment status before another attempt;
no toast or updated total proves bank settlement.

[Operator-added fee](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8430112)

## Configure Stop-to-Stop gratuity

Open the route's **Ticket Management** and review the intended supported checkout
flow. Operator chooses agreed preset percentages, custom-amount availability and
optional/required selection using actual offered controls, then saves. Reopen
these settings and compare saved choices. Do not invent percentage defaults,
submit a customer booking or charge to test them; configuration is not collected
gratuity. If current controls do not match, ask Support rather than enabling a
similarly named setting for a different route experience.

[Gratuity configuration](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8430112)

## Troubleshoot passenger access

The published passenger-account guide uses a company browser link/QR. The August
2026 webinar also announces an app-store experience **and** link access. Public
descriptions therefore differ; confirm the current company-provided experience
instead of asserting either "browser only" or a mandatory app download. Do not
invent an app-store listing, install software or switch identities to test it.
Verify intended company/experience before diagnosing access. September 2026
describes email sign-in/account creation alongside SMS and SSO. That dated
capability statement does not prove all three are enabled for this company or
give an Operator authentication-settings path. Use only the method shown on the
intended company page; do not configure a method, create a passenger account or
switch identities to test it. Ask the provider/Moovs if the expected choice is
absent. Enablement, SSO setup, email credential/verification details and account
recovery are documentation gaps; never invent a password-reset or account-merge
procedure. Passenger signs in and completes verification privately. Pause
inspection for credentials, codes and sensitive account details; never ask for
them in chat. Check correct number/email and use the newest code; allow a short
time for delivery if it has not arrived. Check session state and reopen the
original link if needed; approved-user restrictions go to the provider. Logging
out differs from deleting the account. Label successful access passenger-reported,
not independently verified, unless authorized non-sensitive readback is available.

## Troubleshoot passenger booking

Check current company link, route type, valid date/stops/address eligibility and
validation messages without creating a test booking. Stop-to-Stop paid changes
and Shared Shuttle changes go through the provider. Continuous Loop needs no
seat booking. Stop Time Select cancellation may return a ticket, not refund
package money. Compare the existing booking/confirmation after any operator-led
correction; an empty search or cancelled ticket does not establish a refund.

[Passenger booking](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/9302607)

## Correct missing Shuttle manifest details

Open route > **Bookings**, locate the source booking and compare the missing
passenger, luggage, notes, phone, flight or ADA information. Operator corrects
only the needed source fields, saves and reopens that booking. Coordinate with
the driver to recheck the manifest; a saved booking is not proof their device
updated. Do not expose whole manifests or change a driver's identity.

Driver App **Shuttling** shows **View Stop Plan** before, **View All Stops** during
and **View Stop History** after a trip. Driver handles Boarded/No Show/Disembarked,
undo, scans and departure. QR scanning requires **Confirm Check-In** and updates
occupancy. Never fabricate check-ins or depart stops to clear unresolved counts.

[Source booking correction](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8430112),
[Manifest details and driver-only actions](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/16450762)
