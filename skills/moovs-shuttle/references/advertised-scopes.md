# Additional advertised Shuttle scopes

Reviewed 2026-10-16 against the public [Shuttle product page](https://www.moovsapp.com/shuttle-app).
These are specific Moovs marketing claims, not supported setup/control walkthroughs
or confirmed availability. They do not override Help's Enterprise-only wording,
pricing's cross-tier add-on ambiguity, actual purchased product/role or Free
payment restrictions. Ask which company, Shuttle experience, route/program and
operation is intended; reuse known context rather than auditing unrelated settings.

## Bulk template propagation

The page advertises reusable route templates, bulk trip generation and applying
changes across multiple routes. Editing one route/version or reviewing daily
Operations does not establish these batch operations. **Documentation gap:**
exact target selection, propagation/scheduling effects, approval/submission and
result/error handling. Ask Moovs for the supported instructions and actual affected
scope before changes; never apply a template broadly to test the claim.

## Shuttle wallet payments

The Passenger App copy names an integrated wallet alongside credit cards.
A saved card, ticket package or remaining ticket uses is not proof of a wallet.
**Documentation gap:** supported wallet type, eligibility, funding/use/refund
controls and actual payment effects. Do not invent a wallet balance/top-up flow,
create a Stripe account, purchase a ticket or route Free to payment links/external
Paid marking. Seek supported wallet guidance; passenger owns any private checkout.

## Route delay and change notifications

The product page says riders receive delay/change notifications. Separate
[workplace](https://www.moovsapp.com/post/why-your-workplace-needs-modern-shuttle-tracking)
and [campus](https://www.moovsapp.com/post/why-your-campus-needs-a-shuttle-app)
posts also advertise automatic driver notices when routes change. Check the
intended rider versus driver audience; neither is established by booking SMS,
nearest-shuttle alerts or a displayed delay.
**Documentation gap:** configuration, intended audience/channel, trigger and
reschedule/update effects. Establish the intended notification and current supported
guidance before an operator action; do not change a real route or send a message
to discover who receives it. A display update is not delivery proof.

April separately announces **Shared Shuttle passenger** notices when the
shuttle approaches a stop, arrives, or the booking is canceled. Those event and
route-type scopes are not established by the delay/change claim or a Loop map.
**Documentation gap:** the announcement supplies no supported configuration,
trigger/channel or delivery walkthrough. Ask Moovs for the intended Shared
Shuttle experience before changes; do not cancel a booking or move a real shuttle
to manufacture a notice.

## Capacity overflow alerts

The Driver App marketing names automatic capacity updates and overflow alerts.
Manifest counts, QR boarding and remaining seats do not establish alert controls.
**Documentation gap:** threshold/trigger, recipient/channel, enablement and handling.
Review the actual service/capacity question without boarding a test passenger or
changing capacity/status to force an alert; obtain supported alert instructions.

## Driver navigation and traffic handoff

The Driver App copy advertises optimized turn-by-turn navigation and real-time
traffic. It does not show whether directions open an external maps app or a
Moovs-native navigation/optimization control. **Documentation gap:** supported
entrypoint, route/traffic selection and effect on the intended trip. Driver uses
their own device safely while stationary; operator/assistant do not impersonate
them, change routes or infer automatic retiming/dispatch from traffic. Obtain the
current supported handoff rather than inventing a route-optimization switch.

No action in this reference is a completed setup workflow. Current exact controls,
plan availability, saved outcomes and recipient delivery remain unverified.
