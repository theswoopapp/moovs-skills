# Create an initial Loop or Fixed Schedule route

Reviewed 2026-10-16. Current public [route Help](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8430098)
provides entry, route families and submission. The official-linked
[December 2025 recording](https://fast.wistia.com/embed/iframe/2cr38q63dr?wtime=215s)
shows a literal screen-shared Operator demonstration, including input forms,
Create Route, the resulting route list and reopened route details. This is not
the June illustrated training, but its account/environment is not independently
established and it is not current production or browser acceptance. Match the
present form to Help; pause at a material difference rather than force old labels.

This expands the earlier caption-only timing preparation for **Build and publish
the right route**, not a new counted feature. Historical source outcomes stay intact.
It supplies the ordinary initial Loop/Fixed creation chain. The whole multi-type
scope remains incomplete: Stop-to-Stop pricing/return setup and detailed Shared
configuration still need their own supported choices. Creating a route does not
configure tickets, clients/imports, schedule Shared bookings or prove bookability.

## Establish the intended service

Reuse the company/account and purchased Shuttle/role checks from the skill.
Confirm a new route is intended, the existing company, route name/type, stop
locations/order, approved calendar/timing and vehicles. Do not duplicate a route
to repair a missing operation. Human enters every value, uses every Next and
submits creation. Intermediate setup can have persistence effects; do not assume
closing the builder rolls it back. No real booking, message or departure is a test.
Without authorized read-only browser access, readback is operator-reported.

## Enter Route Details and Stops

1. Use **Shuttle Routes > Create shuttle route**, as current Help directs.
   In the demonstrated **Route Details**, enter **Route Name**, select the
   existing **Company**, optionally enter **Description**, and choose the intended
   **Route Type**. Do not copy the demonstration's names or business values.
2. For **Continuous Loop**, select that type and continue to **Stops**. The
   demonstrated Loop Configuration has **Estimated Loop Duration (minutes)**,
   **Desired Frequency (minutes)** and **Default Dwell Time (minutes)**. Use
   approved values, not the displayed defaults; desired frequency is not a
   guarantee a vehicle can meet that headway.
3. For each Loop stop, select the intended **Stop Address**, enter **Stop Name**
   and dwell time, then travel minutes to the next stop. Include the final stop's
   travel back to the first. Dwell is waiting at the stop, not travel. Use
   **Add Another Stop** as needed and confirm the displayed order. Reordering or
   deletion is an operator change, not read-only inspection.
4. For **Fixed Schedule**, the demonstrated **Stops** stage uses stop addresses
   and names in visit order; its per-stop arrival/departure times are entered
   with the vehicle in the next stage, not as Loop travel/dwell intervals.
   Current Help separately distinguishes Stop Time Select and Stop-to-Stop
   Search. Confirm the intended experience; this dated base route demonstration
   does not teach Stop Groups & Pricing, optional return pairing or ticket setup.
   An employer/guest-sponsored purpose is not proof of fare-free booking; the
   later required-purchase example remains a separate unresolved configuration.
5. Human advances after checking the entries. If the actual form requires an
   undocumented decision or shows validation, resolve that specific issue from
   current supported guidance; do not invent constraints or change requirements.

## Configure Route Version & Vehicles

1. Enter the approved **Start Date** and any intended **End Date**. The dated
   form labels End Date optional; that is not a promise of unlimited service or
   current open-ended/overnight support. Compare the saved period afterward,
   including any generated end date, rather than assuming a blank stays blank.
2. For Loop, select operating weekdays and each day's start/end hours. For
   Fixed Schedule, select the intended days of operation. A weekday example is
   not a restriction against weekends.
3. The demonstrated **Assign First Vehicle** opens a route-instance vehicle
   selection; **Add Additional Vehicle** adds another instance. Select the actual
   suitable vehicle for each intended instance and check its displayed capacity,
   not just the vehicle's name. Resolve incomplete-assignment indications before
   proceeding; do not assert a complete validation contract from this example.
4. For Loop, **Use the route operating hours** can keep a vehicle on the route
   hours. When a vehicle has different coverage, the demonstrated unchecked
   choice exposes its own days/hours. Set only that vehicle's approved exception;
   do not change the entire route to a peak-only vehicle's hours. Current Help
   also allows driver breaks where shown; an undocumented break/overnight choice
   needs supported guidance, not a guessed value.
5. For Fixed Schedule, enter the selected vehicle instance's **Arrival Time**
   and **Departure Time** at each stop. Compare the approved chronology at all
   stops; repeat for additional intended instances. Do not replace these times
   with Loop intervals or infer a maximum/minimum from the demo's examples.

## Review, human creation and saved-state readback

1. In **Review & Create**, compare company/name/type, service period, operating
   days, all stops in order, timing and vehicle instances with the approved plan.
   For Loop, inspect **Stop Schedule Preview** by intended service day, including
   any vehicle-specific exception. A preview is not saved service or a promise
   that desired headway is achieved. Human returns to correct discrepancies.
2. The dated capture shows **Create Route**, followed by the new active route in
   the list and reopened route details for both Loop and Fixed Schedule. Current
   Help explicitly directs **Create Route or Publish** after review. Human uses
   the actual matching submission only after review; do not invent a second
   activation step or infer every route type publishes identically.
3. Reopen the intended saved route. Compare name/company/type, active state,
   service dates including end date, operating days, stops and vehicle assignments.
   Read **Operations** for the intended service date and compare scheduled vehicles
   and times; a toast/active badge alone is insufficient. If no operational dates
   exist, check route-version/calendar/vehicle setup before sharing the link.
4. If authorized, privately check the correct existing passenger experience/link
   and timetable without booking or sending it. A newly active route alone does
   not prove location freshness, passenger boarding, fare setup or collected money.
   On uncertain submission inspect the saved route/list before retrying; never
   repeat Create Route blindly. Report missing readback rather than claim success.
