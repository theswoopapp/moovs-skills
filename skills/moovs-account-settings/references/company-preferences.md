# Company and preferences

Reviewed 2026-10-16. Follow task-authority.md for coached versus executed steps.

## Company details

The published route is **Settings > General > Company**; older navigation opens
Settings from **More**. Ask the operator to review existing business entries
before changing them, because these details are reused in customer confirmations.
Confirm the intended field and replacement, not a wholesale account reset.
After their save, reopen the relevant section and compare only that field.
Do not send a test confirmation to validate branding without a separate task.

Source: [Company Settings](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6365635-company-settings)

## Pricing layout and order types

Pricing Layout defines itemized pricing defaults; Order Types defines service
reasons, not One-Way/Hourly trip types. Choose the intended task below. These are
company defaults, not a way to change one existing booking silently.

Source: [Preferences Settings](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6369519-preferences-settings)

## Edit the pricing layout

The official Training Center links a public **General: Preferences** video.
Its older interface demonstrates **Settings > General > Preferences**:

1. Operator opens **Add Pricing** and chooses the intended available item. The
   demonstration shows preset items and an **Other** item with a custom name.
2. Review dollar versus percentage mode, amount and calculation basis before
   operator entry. A percentage changes price, not just its displayed label;
   use the subtotal guidance below rather than assuming every item uses base rate.
3. Operator changes only the agreed item. Removal via its trash control is also
   consequential, not a preview. The video shows a **Saved** indicator; do not
   assume edits wait for a final Save button before becoming effective.
4. Reopen Preferences and compare item name, mode, value and order. When a
   permitted synthetic quote preview is available, compare its pricing breakdown
   without saving/sending a booking. Saved settings do not prove historic trips
   were repriced, a customer approved the change or any money was collected.

Visual source: [General: Preferences training](https://share.vidyard.com/watch/ak5MvgBXNjRsST6zU2GpfD)
(roughly 00:30-01:25; public video reviewed 2026-10-15), linked by the
[official Training Center](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/8129384-moovs-training-center).
This supports the demonstrated editor, not every current validation or deletion
effect. Stop on a material interface difference instead of trying live changes.

## Choose order types

November2025 explicitly placed **Order Type at reservation level**, not a separate
choice for each trip, and said users could not create their own options then.
Trip Type instead described One-Way/Round-Trip/Hourly service structure. These are
historical limits, not a blanket prohibition on today's custom/per-trip controls.
Check current Help and offered scope before operator edits; request current
supported guidance for absent choices rather than inventing an editor or changing
all trips. [November2025, 38:35-39:25](https://fast.wistia.com/embed/iframe/iwtmxjqqp3?wtime=2315s).

In **Settings > General > Preferences > Order Types**, operator reviews the
business's offered service reasons and changes only the intended checkboxes.
Public help says options start checked; inspect actual values rather than reset
them all. The training video demonstrates narrowing the list and shows the
selection in quote/customer booking forms. It is not driver assignment or pricing.

Reopen Preferences and compare the intended choices. If previewing a permitted
synthetic form, check its Order Type list without submitting a quote/booking.
Do not promise old reservations changed or create a real booking to test a list.

Sources: [Preferences Settings](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6369519-preferences-settings),
[public training](https://share.vidyard.com/watch/ak5MvgBXNjRsST6zU2GpfD)
(roughly 01:25-02:35; exact current options may differ).

The February 2026 update additionally distinguishes **optional** Order Type from
**disabled** Order Type: optional does not require a selection; disabled removes
the dropdown from both Operator and Customer Portal. This differs from unchecking
one service reason. Operator reviews the intended mode before changing it; reopen
the preference and permitted unsubmitted forms on both surfaces. Do not infer
historical orders were rewritten. [February, 07:37-08:16](https://fast.wistia.com/embed/iframe/4xlf6w2fzq?wtime=457s).

## Operational start and end times
The February public demo describes a Preferences control for estimated
garage-based start/end times, with **Pro/Enterprise** access at that recording.
Confirm current account entitlement and garage setup; this is not an inspection
buffer or a customer pickup-time change. Operator enables the intended preference
and shows the relevant start/end columns in Dispatch using actual offered controls.

The narration applies automatic calculation to **new trips created after enabling**.
Read back the saved preference/column choices and, for permitted synthetic new
trip data, compare garage departure, customer pickup and estimated garage return.
Do not shift customer times, assume existing trips recalculated or create a real
booking to test it. A displayed estimate is not live GPS or proof the driver left.
[February, 08:33-09:59](https://fast.wistia.com/embed/iframe/4xlf6w2fzq?wtime=513s).

September2026 separately previews customizable BRA start/end times as upcoming.
The estimate preference above does not establish that editor or its release;
obtain current supported instructions for the intended BRA timing change.
[September, 49:31-50:02](https://fast.wistia.com/embed/iframe/zhhho37xt1?wtime=2971s).

## Dedicated fees and payment-method changes

April's demo adds **Credit card service fee**, **Standard transportation cost** and
custom **Other 4-6** pricing items through **Add Pricing**. Configured default
amounts can populate newly created trip/quote pricing. Review item, dollar/percent
mode and base-rate versus subtotal position; do not copy the example rate or
promise old bookings changed. Operator edits defaults; reopen and compare them.

A card-service-fee line is **not** an automatic payment-method detector. The
demonstrator manually removes that line on an exact reservation before recording
cash. Confirm the business-approved fee/receipt treatment before operator pricing
correction; compare line items and revised total before any separate payment action.
Free still cannot mark external-method trip payments Paid. Do not remove fees,
record receipts, refund/recharge or promise surcharge legality automatically.
[April, 11:20-15:34](https://fast.wistia.com/embed/iframe/krp2b1wij8?wtime=680s),
[Free recording limit](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6415309-processing-a-payment).

## Default extra-stop price

For a bounded review, open **Settings > General > Preferences** and inspect
**Enter in a price per stop**. Compare the current amount and its displayed
transfer/base-rate help with the business's intended default; do not copy a demo rate.
The [official Preferences training](https://share.vidyard.com/watch/ak5MvgBXNjRsST6zU2GpfD)
shows $0 (00:45–01:20). The [December 2025 webinar](https://fast.wistia.com/embed/iframe/2cr38q63dr?wtime=2517s)
shows an existing $25 value and matching help that includes additional transfer
stops in base rate (41:57–42:40). Its narration describes an added stop on a
one-way Customer Portal trip adding the example $25; it is not an hourly rule.

These recordings support locating/reviewing the default and that scoped effect,
not a demonstrated edit/save/readback of this field. Before coaching a change,
obtain current official field-persistence guidance; do not invent a final Save
button or infer persistence from the page's existing Saved indicator. After an
authorized operator change, reopen and compare the intended value. A permitted
unsubmitted synthetic transfer can check the requested pricing effect separately.
Do not infer every stop-counting/zone-pricing rule, a separate fee line or
historical repricing; escalate only the unresolved calculation relevant to the task.

## Driver Amount Due visibility

For review, open **Settings > General > Preferences** and inspect
**Enable Amount Due on driver app** and its current state/help. The
[official Preferences training](https://share.vidyard.com/watch/ak5MvgBXNjRsST6zU2GpfD)
(00:45–01:30) and [December 2025 webinar](https://fast.wistia.com/embed/iframe/2cr38q63dr?wtime=2520s)
(42:00–42:40) show Off with disabled help. The label identifies a visibility
preference, not payment collection or permission for a driver to charge.

Neither recording demonstrates changing this toggle, its save transition or an
enabled driver-side result. Confirm the intended visibility and obtain current
official change/persistence guidance before operator changes; do not borrow a
save sequence from another pricing item. Reopen the intended saved toggle after
an authorized change. Claiming an actual driver-side outcome additionally needs
a permitted synthetic check by the authorized driver, not impersonation or a
real passenger balance. That check is not required merely to explain the setting.

## Enable existing luggage counters

The [April 2025 webinar](https://fast.wistia.com/embed/iframe/bdlvo3sv3y?wtime=702s),
linked by the [official library](https://www.moovsapp.com/post/moovs-webinars),
records **General > Preferences > Enable luggage options for orders** changing
from Off through **Saving** to On/**Saved** at 12:13–12:22. This is literal dated
recorded UI, not a current Operator inspection; stop if actual controls differ.

1. Confirm the company, relevant access and intended customer-facing visibility.
   Operator changes only the matching luggage toggle. It saves as part of that
   interaction; do not treat the toggle as a preview or invent a final Save step.
2. Wait for the actual save outcome, then reopen Preferences and compare the
   intended enabled state. Uncertain saving is not permission to toggle again.
3. In a permitted **unsubmitted synthetic** Customer Portal form, inspect the
   luggage fields. At 13:24–13:28 the recording shows Carry-On, Checked and Oversize
   counters, with selections reflected in Additional Trip Info. Use those as
   dated examples, not universal current types; counter edits need approved business values and task scope.

These steps enable/review existing counters, not custom luggage-type or limit
configuration, a Shuttle fare or vehicle-capacity enforcement. Do not submit a
booking/payment to test them or infer historical orders changed. Saved settings
and any operator-reported form result are not independent live acceptance.

## Subtotal-based percentage items

The March 2026 public demonstration shows moving percentage items such as tax
and gratuity below a subtotal so the calculation includes prior itemized amounts,
not only base rate. This changes the calculation, not just presentation.

Before the operator reorders or changes a pricing element, confirm the intended
basis and applicable business policy; do not invent tax or gratuity rules. Review
the current layout, item ordering and calculated breakdown. For a fictional base
of 100 and an included fee of 100, a 7% subtotal item would be 14 rather than 7;
this illustrates arithmetic only, not a recommended rate or taxable basis.

Layout edits/saves follow task authority. Reopen the setting and inspect a
permitted synthetic preview/breakdown. The demonstration says the layout applies
across trip types, but does not establish retroactive repricing of saved bookings.
Do not create a real booking, change customer prices or charge to test it.
