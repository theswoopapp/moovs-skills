# Set and review Customer Portal vehicle display order

Reviewed 2026-10-16. Current public Help establishes sort choices; September2025
literal product-training footage supplies the matching controls and a bounded
customer preview. Neither is current authenticated Operator acceptance.
Operator performs every selection, dropdown, drag and preview entry.

1. Confirm company, access and approved display policy: price, capacity, name or
   a specific manual sequence. Open **Settings > Customer Portal > Settings**
   and scroll to **Vehicle Order**. This changes customer-facing presentation,
   not fare calculation, vehicle availability or dispatch assignment priority.
2. For **By price**, **By capacity** or **By name**, operator selects the intended
   radio option and its **Ascending/Descending** dropdown. Ascending price means
   lowest first; ascending capacity means smallest first. Do not choose a price
   strategy for the business or promise every unpriced vehicle's position.
3. For **Manual**, operator selects its radio option and drags only intended
   vehicle rows using their left-hand handles into the agreed relative order.
   In the recording the page shows **Saved** with no separate Save button used
   for these changes. Treat selections/dragging as consequential, not unsaved
   staging. If current persistence controls differ, obtain supported instructions.
4. Reopen Settings and compare the selected method/direction or manual sequence.
   If a non-submitting display preview is authorized, operator opens
   **Installation & Share > Customer Portal Link > Open Link**, enters synthetic
   trip details/group size, and proceeds only to **Choose Vehicle**. Stop before
   vehicle choice, customer identity, booking/request submission or payment.
5. Compare the request summary, visible capacities, active **Min Capacity** filter
   and eligible list order. September shows a group-size filter already applied;
   smaller vehicles can therefore be absent without being hidden/deleted. Its
   chip has a remove control and narration says customers can remove it, so it is
   not proof of a capacity safety lock or permission to overload a vehicle.
   Interpret the filter before diagnosing order; do not disable other protections
   or change real bookings to force a test. Preview is not saved booking evidence.

Reconcile unexpected display against actual portal visibility, pricing settings,
cutoff, filters and current guidance. Do not activate BRA or change prices merely
to make sorting appear to work. No cross-device/persistence guarantee follows
from the recording's Saved indicator; compare actual reopened state.
