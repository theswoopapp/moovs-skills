# Policies, branding and sharing

Reviewed 2026-10-16. Changes follow [task authority](task-authority.md). Guidance explains controls,
not legal advice, permission to publish policies, or payment/refund execution.

## Terms and customer signature

1. Obtain the operator-approved business wording; do not invent legal terms.
2. Public guide: **More > Settings > Terms and Conditions > Create Term**.
   Operator selects term, enters wording, **Save Term**; reopen and compare text.
3. Under **Settings > Customer Portal > Settings > Customer Signature**, at least
   one Terms & Conditions document is needed before enabling. Operator chooses
   **Require consent** (checkbox) or **Require consent and signature** (checkbox
   plus drawn signature) as intended. This requires agreement to the company's
   Terms & Conditions and Cancellation Policy before booking; the documented
   setup prerequisite is at least one Terms document.
4. Verify saved mode and preview customer acknowledgement without entering
   another person's signature or completing a booking.

Sources: [Terms](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6414537-terms-conditions),
[Portal Settings](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6365893-customer-portal-settings).

## Retrieve an existing signed terms PDF

Historical narrated procedure, reviewed 2026-10-16: the April2025 webinar names
**reservation > More > View PDFs > signed Terms and Conditions**; the2022 video
also describes downloading signed terms. This is caption evidence, not a newly
inspected screen, universal plan entitlement or proof that every booking has one.

1. Identify the company and existing reservation by reference/date/contact. Confirm
   the operator is authorized to access its documents and that the goal is to
   retrieve existing terms, not enable signatures or make a new booking.
2. On that reservation, have the operator use the matching **More > View PDFs**
   controls and locate the signed Terms PDF. If missing or different, stop and
   obtain current Moovs instructions; do not recreate a plausible menu or promise
   a document was generated. A booking existing does not prove consent was signed.
3. Operator alone downloads/opens the intended file privately. Keep signatures,
   IP addresses and other personal details out of assistant inspection/chat.
   They compare the reservation, policy text/version, signer and available date/
   signature evidence with their own records. A mismatch needs private review,
   not editing someone else's signature or deciding that the booking is fraudulent.
4. Ask only whether the intended existing PDF was retrieved and matched, not for
   the file or personal contents. Report operator-confirmed unless independently
   verified within authorized, non-sensitive scope. Download is not proof of legal
   enforceability, dispute success, payment, or delivery to anyone else.

Do not create a test booking, charge a deposit, enable a global signature setting,
sign for a customer, or send the PDF to exercise this retrieval workflow.

Sources: [April2025,47:42–48:51](https://fast.wistia.com/embed/iframe/bdlvo3sv3y?wtime=2862s),
[2022,02:10–02:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QzSivKy5wK8&t=130s).

## Cancellation policies

1. **Settings > Cancellation > Create Policy**. Operator selects intended
   Flexible, Moderate or Strict level; these are configurable business choices.
2. Review Full/50%/25% refund conditions, notice quantities and Hours/Days/Weeks.
   Do not treat 30 days and four weeks as equivalent or suggest a policy percentage.
   Operator turns unused 50%/25% conditions off as agreed; an enabled tier is
   not the same as offering no refund at that tier.
3. Operator saves policy and explicitly associates the correct vehicles through
   their Cancellation dropdown. Read back both policy and vehicle association.
   With no vehicles yet, save the policy first; associate and verify it when the
   intended vehicle is created. Until then, association remains unfinished.
   This selection determines the policy sent with the Reservation Confirmation
   email; saved association does not prove that any email was sent or delivered.
4. Policy wording/refund eligibility is not an executed refund. Handling an
   individual cancellation or refund requires a separate exact-record task.

[Cancellation Policy](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6399567-cancellation-policy),
[vehicle policy association](https://intercom.help/moovs-05c940f1970e/en/articles/6963946-moovs-vehicle-setup-and-pricing-terminology).

## Branding

Logo and booking-tool banner are separate assets. Older labels may differ;
stop if the current control cannot be identified, rather than editing a website.

July's narration locates portal **Branding**, changes its color and refreshes the
customer portal to inspect the result. For that bounded color task, confirm the
intended company/color; operator uses only matching current supported controls.
The narration does not establish a final save/autosave sequence: reconcile that
before editing, then reopen the setting and refresh an authorized non-submitting
portal preview to compare color. Preview is not a booking or a sent confirmation.

The same recording says the portal background picture does not display on mobile,
while the logo does. Review desktop/mobile presentation separately; a missing
mobile background is not by itself an upload failure. Keep color, company logo,
portal background/banner and website assets distinct; stop if current labels or
behavior differ instead of replacing another asset.

## Replace or remove the company logo

Public guide: **Settings > General**. Operator supplies an authorized image and
uses the current upload/replace/remove control, then reopens to compare the
intended logo. Do not replace an unrelated banner or website asset.

## Replace or remove the booking-tool banner

Public guide: **Settings > Booking Tool**. Operator uses the identified banner
upload/replace/remove control and reopens its saved result. Use actual current
labels; do not infer this is the company logo or a website-builder image.

## General portal link and QR code

The public business link and a saved QR image are distinct artifacts.

## Copy the general Customer Portal link

**Settings > Customer Portal > Installation & Share > Customer Portal Link**:
operator copies the general business link, not a customer reservation/payment
link. Confirm intended company and destination before public distribution.
Copying does not send it to anyone.

## Create and verify a business QR code

1. **Settings > Moovs Market > QR Codes > Create a New QR Code**.
   Operator supplies name and intended URL. Optional **UTM Parameters** add
   campaign name, source, term or campaign ID. Review the full destination
   preview without customer identifiers; attribution requires analytics that
   records those parameters. A saved code or scan alone is not analytics proof.
2. Operator selects **Generate**, opens saved code and downloads image. Scan the
   image on a device and compare resolved destination before printing/sharing.
3. To remove an intended saved code, operator reviews the exact code and uses
   its actions to remove it from the Moovs list, then checks that list. This
   does not disable its destination URL or downloaded/printed copies; do not
   promise revocation or delete a code merely to test access.
4. Optional external alternate, from older guidance: operator selects the
   generator's **Open a Website** function, pastes the copied general business
   Portal link, then uses **Create QR Code**. Review the resulting destination
   before distribution. Do not upload customer/reservation/payment links; use
   current supported controls rather than assuming native **Generate** labels.

## Customer journey and confirmation

Public guidance describes in-app confirmation, not a configurable external thank-you
redirect. For conversion tracking, consult current public GTM guidance rather
than inventing a redirect or marking a browser page view as a completed booking.

The July narration describes customer pickup selection as date, specific time
and **AM/PM**, with an AM confirmation for midnight-to-six-AM choices. Relay this
to the customer in their own identity; ask them to compare their intended date,
time and meridiem before proceeding. This is field-selection guidance, not an
inspected current control, timezone/cutoff validation or a completed booking.
If the offered picker differs, obtain current guidance rather than inventing it;
do not submit a reservation to test the confirmation.

## Help customers review a reservation

For customer-owned history, favorite/status review and an intended repeat booking,
use [Customer Portal Trips history](customer-trip-history.md). This adds the
Trips workflow to this same customer-review scope, not Passenger App access or
permission for the operator/assistant to book as that customer.

Customers can follow their own reservation confirmation link to review it.
For account/history access, relay these steps to the customer in their own identity:
1. Customer opens the intended company's portal and enters their mobile number
   or email address in the login field.
2. Customer receives the verification code by SMS for mobile or by email.
3. Customer privately enters that code to access their own account.
The customer—not the operator or assistant—performs these login actions. Never
request or relay their code, inspect sensitive entry, or claim customer acceptance
from an operator preview. Do not open private customer links merely to demonstrate.
The public Reservation Review page names **Passenger**, **Request a change**
for date/time changes and **View Price Summary**. These are customer actions,
not authority for an operator/assistant to sign in as that customer or pay.
For unpriced reservation requests, customers use their own **Customer Portal >
Orders** and the offered status filters. **Choose Vehicle** without a price
describes that request journey, not proof of acceptance or payment. Operator
review uses **Reservations > Unconfirmed** instead; this is not Passenger App
Trips. Submission/acceptance does not automatically charge a card, although
other portal settings can still require payment information. A later charge is a
separate operator-reviewed task; an email/status alone is not its receipt.
