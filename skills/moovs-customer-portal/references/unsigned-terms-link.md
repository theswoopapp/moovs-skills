# Copy and review the unsigned company terms link

Reviewed 2026-10-16 from literal October2024 product-training footage and narration,
not current production UI. This copies an existing policy link; it does not create
terms, obtain consent, generate a signed PDF or send anything to a customer.

1. Confirm the intended company and that the approved terms already exist.
   Operator opens **Settings > Terms & Conditions**, then an existing term.
   Opening its details is not permission to edit its text or create another term.
2. In the matching term drawer, operator opens the upper-right **three-dot menu**
   and chooses **Terms & Conditions** under **COPY**. Do not choose **Delete Term**.
   The demonstrated copy produces a link to the company's formatted terms page,
   including multiple sections, not an individual signed booking document.
3. Operator opens the copied link in a browser and compares company and approved
   policy wording across the intended sections. Check the destination itself;
   a copied-link toast alone does not prove the right terms were obtained.
   If controls or destination differ, stop for current supported guidance.
4. If sharing is separately requested, operator reviews the exact recipient,
   channel, destination and message before sending. Copying/opening is not send
   authorization or evidence of delivery. Keep private booking links out of chat.

The demonstrated page is unsigned. Do not describe a shared link as customer
acceptance, a retrospective signature on an existing reservation, or a substitute
for the separate Customer Signature booking flow. For an existing signed record,
use [signed-PDF retrieval](policies-sharing.md#retrieve-an-existing-signed-terms-pdf).
No signature mode change or test reservation is needed to copy these terms.
