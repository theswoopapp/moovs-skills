# Customer Portal Trips history and repeat bookings

Reviewed 2026-10-16 from literal February/August2025 public product recordings
and current public customer-account guidance. Dated training, not current tenant
acceptance. This is the browser Customer Portal, not Passenger App Trips or an
Operator Duplicate Trip/Reservation action. No universal plan entitlement is
established here; use the intended company's actual offered customer controls.

## Review history and repeat an intended service

1. The customer uses their **own** account in the intended company's portal.
   Follow the [customer login guidance](policies-sharing.md#help-customers-review-a-reservation)
   if needed; they privately receive/enter their own email/SMS code. The operator
   can relay steps, never sign in as the customer or ask them to share that code.
2. Customer opens their top-right name menu > **Orders > Trips**. Review
   **Upcoming** versus **Past**. Identify the intended historical trip by its date,
   pickup time/timezone and route, not just its row position or a similar name.
   Use that row's **View** to check the actual trip details before repeating it.
3. Confirm the new service with the operator: customer/passenger, intended new
   dates and local pickup times, ordered addresses/stops, vehicle/service, current
   rate/terms and availability. A previous trip's price or driver is not a promise
   for the new date. If copied details need changes, establish the supported
   change/rebooking path before creating anything; this date dialog is not an
   itinerary or fare editor. Do not create a wrong booking hoping to fix it later.
4. Back on the correct historical row, customer selects **Repeat**. The dated
   **Duplicate Trip** dialog has **Select all dates to duplicate on** and a
   calendar. Customer selects only the agreed dates and compares the complete
   selected-date list/month/year before proceeding; do not accept default dates.
5. Treat **Duplicate** as creation, not a preview: February returns directly to
   Trips and adds upcoming rows. There is no demonstrated second review/checkout
   screen. Resolve any missing current fare, availability or unclear payment/
   request consequence with the operator **before** that action. If the current
   flow offers another review or payment step, customer reviews its actual fields,
   itinerary, amount/terms and effect, privately completes sensitive entry and
   personally submits only the intended service. Do not invent final labels,
   assume no charge, or bypass required validation. Preparation only: stop before
   Duplicate or any other submission.
6. For the agreed new bookings, customer personally submits **Duplicate** once
   when that is the offered final action. Return to **Trips > Upcoming** and use
   **View** on each intended result. Compare its new date/time/timezone, route/
   stops, passenger, vehicle/service and displayed confirmation/request state
   with the approved service. If View omits price, compare that new reservation
   through its **View Price Summary** or ask the operator to check its recorded
   rate; a passenger trip view is not payment/price evidence. Check its new reference
   and leave the historical original unchanged. Row count/toast alone is not
   complete readback; do not assume copied driver, fare or all other fields.
7. Operator checks corresponding new record(s) where authorized if confirmation
   or price is unclear. Keep created, requested, confirmed and paid distinct;
   a new list entry proves neither payment nor driver acceptance. If submission
   times out or a result is missing, inspect existing history/records before
   repeating. Do not duplicate again, cancel or refund as automatic cleanup.

The customer owns every date selection, favorite and submission. Give one step
at a time unless they request the complete checklist. Without permitted direct
readback, report customer/operator-reported rather than independently verified.
Private trip links/details and payment information stay out of chat/public notes.

## Favorite or inspect driver status without repeating

- To keep an intended existing trip easy to find, customer selects its outline
  **star**. February shows a filled star and that trip moving to the top of the
  Past table. Compare the same trip and its favorite state, then revisit Trips
  to check the intended result. This is a recommended persistence check, not a
  filmed refresh guarantee. Favoriting does not create another booking. No
  un-favorite, cross-device or automatic repeat behavior is established here.
- August's Trips table separately displays **Confirmation Status**, **Driver
  Status**, **Driver** and **Passenger**; horizontally scroll the table if needed.
  Match the exact trip first and read each column literally. Confirmation is not
  the driver's progress. The inspected example shows Confirmed; narration gives
  On the Way/On Location as other examples, not observed transitions or a full
  status dictionary. Driver/status display is not proof of GPS freshness, ETA,
  actual arrival, payment or every staff member's access to other customers.
